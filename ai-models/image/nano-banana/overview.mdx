## Overview

Nano Banana is Zoice's fast and efficient AI image generation model, optimized for quick iterations and rapid prototyping. Perfect for users who need fast results without compromising on quality.

## Key Features

### Fast Generation
- **Speed**: 3-5 seconds average
- **Ideal For**: Rapid prototyping, testing prompts, high-volume needs
- **Trade-off**: Slightly less detail than premium models

### Quality Output
- **Resolution**: Up to 2048x2048 pixels
- **Detail Level**: High quality for most use cases
- **Consistency**: Reliable, predictable results

### Versatility
- **Styles**: Photorealistic, artistic, illustrative
- **Subjects**: People, objects, landscapes, abstract
- **Use Cases**: Social media, web graphics, concepts

## Limitations

- **Resolution**: Maximum 2048x2048 (not ideal for print)
- **Detail**: Less fine detail than Imagen 4 Ultra
- **Complexity**: May struggle with very complex scenes
- **Photorealism**: Good but not as refined as premium models

## Comparison with Other Models

| Feature | Nano Banana | Imagen 4 | Imagen 4 Ultra |
|---------|-------------|----------|----------------|
| **Speed** | 3-5 sec | 10-15 sec | 20-30 sec |
| **Max Resolution** | 2048px | 4096px | 8192px |
| **Detail Level** | High | Very High | Ultra High |
| **Cost** | 1 credit | 3 credits | 5 credits |
| **Best For** | Speed & Volume | Balance | Premium Quality |

## When to Use Nano Banana

✅ **Use Nano Banana When:**
- You need fast results
- Working on web/social media content
- Prototyping and testing ideas
- Budget is a consideration
- Generating high volumes
- Learning and experimenting

❌ **Consider Other Models When:**
- Need maximum resolution for print
- Require ultra-fine details
- Creating hero/flagship images
- Professional photography replacement
