## Overview

Nano Banana 2 offers improved quality over standard Nano Banana while maintaining fast generation times, perfect for professional work with quick turnaround.

## Quick Start

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to Image Generator by Clicking on New Image">
    Go to the Image Generator tool in your Zoice dashboard by clicking on New Image.
    <Frame>
      ![New Image](../../../images/navigate-to-new-image.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Nano Banana 2 Model">
    Choose "Nano Banana 2" from the model selector.
    <Frame>
      ![Select Model](../../../images/select-image-model.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure Settings for Image">
    Set resolution and quality for the image you want to generate.
    <Frame>
      ![Configure Settings](../../../images/text-to-image.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write Your Prompt">
    Enter a description of the image you want to create in the prompt box.
    <Frame>
      ![Write Prompt](../../../images/write-prompt-or-upload-reference.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click on Generate">
    Click generate and wait few seconds for your image to be generated.
    <Frame>
      ![Generate](../../../images/generate-your-image.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download">
    Save your generated image
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Best Practices

- Clear, focused prompts
- Include quality keywords
- Leverage speed for iterations
