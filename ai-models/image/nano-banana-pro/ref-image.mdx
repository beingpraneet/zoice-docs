## Overview

Nano Banana Pro supports reference images, allowing you to guide generation with existing images while maintaining fast processing times.

## Quick Start

The AI analyzes your input image and applies transformations based on your text prompt while maintaining the overall composition and structure of the original image.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to Image Generator by Clicking on New Image">
    Go to the Image Generator tool by clicking on **New Image**.
    <Frame>
      ![Navigate to New Image](../../../images/navigate-to-new-image.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Select Nano Banana Pro Model">
    Choose "Nano Banana Pro" from the model selector.
    <Frame>
      ![Select Model](../../../images/select-image-model.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Configure Settings for Image">
    Choose **Image to Image** mode and set your preferred resolution.
    <Frame>
      ![Configure Settings](../../../images/i2i-3.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Upload Reference Image">
    Upload the image you want to use as a base.
    <Frame>
      ![Upload Reference Image](../../../images/i2i-5.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Type Detailed Prompt">
    Enter a description of how you want the AI to modify your reference image.
    <Frame>
      ![Type Detailed Prompt](../../../images/i2i-6.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Generate">
    Click on **Generate** and wait a few seconds for your transformed image to be created.
    <Frame>
      ![Generate](../../../images/i2i-7.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Download">
    Save your generated image.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Best Practices

- Use clear reference images
- Adjust strength for desired effect
- Combine with detailed prompts
