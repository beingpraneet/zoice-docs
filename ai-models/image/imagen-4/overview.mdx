## When to Use Imagen 4?

 **Use Imagen 4 When:**
- Professional quality is required
- Creating marketing materials
- Need photorealistic results
- Print-ready images needed
- Complex scenes required
- Detail is critical

 **Consider Other Models When:**
- Speed is priority (use Nano Banana)
- Maximum resolution needed (use Imagen 4 Ultra)
- Budget is tight (use Nano Banana)
- Testing/prototyping (use Nano Banana)
