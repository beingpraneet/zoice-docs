## Overview

Veo 3.1 delivers enhanced video quality with improved motion and better prompt understanding for professional video creation.

## Quick Start

Generate videos from text prompts using Zoice’s AI video generation models.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to New Video">
    Go to the Video Generator tool by clicking on **New Video**.
    <Frame>
      ![Navigate to New Video](../../../images/new-video.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Select Veo 3.1 Model">
    Choose **Veo 3.1** from the model selector.
    <Frame>
      ![Select Model](../../../images/select-video-model.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Configure Settings for Video">
    Set dimensions, resolution, and choose **Text to Video** mode.
    <Frame>
      ![Configure Settings](../../../images/t2v-4.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Write Your Text Prompt">
    Enter a description of the scene and action you want to create in the prompt box.
    <Frame>
      ![Write Your Text Prompt](../../../images/t2v-6.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Click on Generate">
    Click on the **Generate** button to begin creating your video.
    <Frame>
      ![Generate](../../../images/t2v-7.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Download">
    Save your generated video.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Best Practices

- Use detailed scene descriptions
- Include camera movements
- Specify lighting and mood
- Describe action clearly
