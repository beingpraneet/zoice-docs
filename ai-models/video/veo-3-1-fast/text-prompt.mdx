## Overview

Veo 3.1 Fast provides professional-quality videos with faster generation times, ideal for projects requiring quick turnaround.

## Quick Start

Generate videos from text prompts using Zoice’s AI video generation models.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to New Video">
    Go to the Video Generator tool in your Zoice dashboard by clicking on **New Video**.
    <Frame>
      ![Navigate to New Video](../../../images/new-video.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Select Veo 3.1 Fast Model">
    Choose **Veo 3.1 Fast** from the model selector based on your project needs.
    <Frame>
      ![Select Model](../../../images/select-video-model.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Select Size and Resolution">
    Configure the dimensions and quality settings for your video output.
    <Frame>
      ![Select Size and Resolution](../../../images/t2v-4.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Select Text to Video">
    Choose the **Text to Video** mode to generate dynamic content from your text descriptions.
    <Frame>
      ![Select Text to Video](../../../images/t2v-5.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Write Your Text Prompt">
    Enter a detailed description of the scene and action you want to create in the prompt box.
    <Frame>
      ![Write Your Text Prompt](../../../images/t2v-6.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Click on Generate">
    Click on the **Generate** button to begin creating your video.
    <Frame>
      ![Generate](../../../images/t2v-7.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Best Practices

- Clear scene descriptions
- Include camera movements
- Leverage speed for iterations
