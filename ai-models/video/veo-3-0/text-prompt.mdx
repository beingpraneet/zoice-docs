## Overview

Veo 3.0 creates professional-quality videos from text prompts with realistic motion and excellent visual quality.

## Quick Start

Generate videos from text prompts using Zoice’s AI video generation models.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to New Video">
    Go to the Video Generator tool by clicking on **New Video**.
    <Frame>
      ![Navigate to New Video](../../../images/new-video.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Select Veo 3.0 Model">
    Choose **Veo 3.0** from the model selector.
    <Frame>
      ![Select Model](../../../images/select-video-model.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Configure Settings for Video">
    Set the resolution and screen size for your video output.
    <Frame>
      ![Configure Settings](../../../images/veo3v-3.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Write Your Text Prompt">
    Enter a description of the scene and action you want to create in the prompt box.
    <Frame>
      ![Write Your Text Prompt](../../../images/veo3v-4.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Click on Generate">
    Click on the **Generate** button to start generating your video.
    <Frame>
      ![Generate](../../../images/veo3v-5.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Download">
    Save your generated video.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Best Practices

- Clear scene descriptions
- Include camera movements
- Describe action and motion
- Use cinematic terms
