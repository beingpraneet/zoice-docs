## Overview

Veo 3.0 supports reference images to guide video generation, allowing you to maintain consistent styles and extend from static images.

## Quick Start

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select Veo 3.0">
    Choose from model selector
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload Reference">
    Select reference image
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write Prompt">
    Describe desired motion
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate">
    Create your video
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Best Practices

- Use clear references
- Describe motion clearly
- Specify camera work
- Adjust influence strength
