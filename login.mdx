# Log in to Zoice

This guide explains how to sign in to your Zoice account using your email and password. Follow the steps below to access your dashboard and start creating with Zoice.

If you don’t have an account yet, complete the signup process first.

## Step 1 — Go to the Zoice Login Page

Open the login page:

## Step 2 — Enter your Email Address

Type the email you used to create your Zoice account.

## Step 3 — Enter your Password

Enter your password in the password field.

![Enter Password](../images/Enter your password.png)

## Step 4 — Click “Continue”

Click the **Continue** button to sign in.

If your credentials are correct, you’ll be redirected to your Zoice dashboard.

## Forgot your password?

If you can’t remember your password:

1. Click **Forgot password?** on the login page
2. Enter your email
3. Check your inbox for a reset link
4. Create a new password

If you don’t receive the email, check spam or promotions folders.

## Troubleshooting Login Issues

### Invalid email or password

Double-check the spelling of your email and try resetting your password.

### Didn’t receive reset email

- Check your spam folder
- Ensure you entered the correct email
- Wait 1–2 minutes; email delivery may be delayed

### Account not found

Make sure you used the same email you used during registration.

### Still unable to log in?

Contact support: [**contact@zoice.com**](mailto:contact@zoice.com)
