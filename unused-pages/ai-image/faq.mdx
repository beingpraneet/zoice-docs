## Frequently Asked Questions (AI Images)

Find answers to the most common questions users have about generating AI images on Zoice.

---

## What is the AI Image tool on Zoice?  
The AI Image tool lets you create images by simply describing what you want. The AI turns your text prompts into unique images.

## How do I generate an image using Zoice?  
To generate an image, enter a text prompt describing the image you want. You can also select styles or themes. Then click "Generate" and download your image.

## What kind of prompts work best?  
Clear and specific prompts yield the best results. For example, instead of saying "a cat," try "a black cat sitting on a red sofa in a modern living room."

## Can I customize the style of the generated images?  
Yes. You can choose from different styles, such as realistic, artistic, or abstract, to match your needs.

## How long does it take to generate an image?  
Most images are generated in a few seconds. More complex prompts may take a little longer.

## Are there any limits on how many images I can generate?  
Your ability to generate images may depend on your Zoice subscription plan or available credits. Check your account details for more information.

## What if the generated image doesn't match my prompt?  
Try refining your prompt with more detail or adjusting the style settings. Experimentation can help you get closer to the desired result.

<Alert type="info">
Tip: The more specific you are in your prompt, the better the AI can understand and generate the image you have in mind.
</Alert>
