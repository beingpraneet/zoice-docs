## How to Generate AI Images on Zoice

Generating AI images on Zoice is simple and intuitive. Follow these steps to create stunning visuals using the AI Image tool.

## Step-by-Step Guide

### Step 1: Log In to Zoice
Go to [Zoice.com](https://zoice.com) and log in to your account. If you don't have an account yet, sign up for one.

### Step 2: Navigate to AI Images
Once logged in, you'll see a navigation menu on the left side of the dashboard. Click on **"New Images"** under the AI Images section. You can also find this option as a pop-up on the homepage.

### Step 3: Enter Your Prompt
In the AI Images interface, you'll see a prompt box where you can type a description of the image you want. Be as specific as possible for the best results.

### Step 4: Choose a Model and Resolution
In the top-right corner of the prompt box, you can select from different AI models (like NanoBanana, Imagine 3, Imagine 4, etc.) based on your needs. In the bottom-left corner, choose the resolution or aspect ratio for your image (e.g., square, landscape, portrait).

### Step 5: Generate the Image
After entering your prompt and selecting your options, press the Enter key or click the generate button. The AI will create your image in a few seconds.

### Step 6: Download and Use Your Image
Once your image is generated, you can preview it and download it for your use. You're all set!

<Alert type="info">
Tip: For best results, experiment with different prompts and models to see the range of images Zoice can generate.
