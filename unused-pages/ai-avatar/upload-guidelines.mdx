## Avatar Upload Guidelines

To create a high-quality avatar on Zoice, it’s important to start with the right kind of image. Here are the guidelines to help you choose and upload the best images for your avatar creation.

## Choosing the Right Image

**1. Use High-Resolution Photos**  
Upload images that are clear and high-resolution. Ideally, images should be at least **1024x1024 pixels** to ensure that the avatar looks crisp and detailed.

**2. Good Lighting and Clarity**  
Make sure the photo is well-lit and not blurry. Natural lighting is best. Avoid dark or grainy photos, as they can result in less accurate avatars.

**3. Front-Facing Images**  
Use images where the person is facing the camera directly. This helps the AI capture facial features accurately. Avoid images where the face is turned away or partially hidden.

**4. Minimal Accessories**  
If possible, choose images with minimal accessories like hats or large sunglasses. This ensures the AI can accurately recreate facial details.

## Uploading Your Image

**Step 1: Navigate to Manage Avatars**  
Go to the **"Manage Avatars"** section in Zoice.

**Step 2: Click on "Create New"**  
Select the option to create a new avatar and choose the **"Upload Image"** method.

**Step 3: Upload Your Chosen Image**  
Click the upload button and select your high-quality, front-facing photo. Once uploaded, you can name your avatar and proceed to customization.

## Image Quality Tips

- **Resolution**: The higher the resolution, the better the avatar. Aim for images that are not pixelated or stretched.  
- **Lighting**: Even lighting on the face helps the AI understand features better.  
- **Facial Expression**: Neutral or slight smiles work best for a versatile avatar.
