## Overview

Generate personalized AI avatars by providing text descriptions. This feature allows you to create unique avatars based on detailed textual prompts.

## How to Create a Text-Based Avatar

1. Navigate to the **AI Avatar** section
2. Select **Custom Avatar** > **Text to Avatar**
3. Enter a detailed description of your desired avatar
4. Click **Generate Avatar**

## Best Practices

- Be specific with your descriptions
- Include details about appearance, style, and characteristics
- Use clear and descriptive language
- Experiment with different prompts for varied results

## Example Prompts

```
A professional business avatar with short brown hair, wearing a navy blue suit
```

```
A friendly avatar with glasses, curly red hair, and a warm smile
```

## Tips for Better Results

- Describe facial features clearly
- Specify clothing and accessories
- Mention the desired mood or expression
- Include background preferences if needed
