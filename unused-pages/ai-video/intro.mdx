## AI Video Generator Introduction

Welcome to the AI Video Generator on Zoice! This guide will walk you through the interface and show you how to create your first AI-generated video step by step.

## Interface Overview

<Steps>

### Step 1: Access the Video Creation Panel
Once you log in to Zoice, click on the **"New Video"** option at the top-right corner of the dashboard. This will open the AI Video interface.

### Step 2: Using the Prompt Box and Model Selection
In the center of the interface, you'll see a **prompt box**. Here, you can type in a description of the video you want to create. 

On the top-right corner of the prompt box, you can select the model version. Options might include **Veo 2, Veo 3, Veo 3.1 Fast,** and others. Each model may produce slightly different results.

### Step 3: Check Your Credits
In the top-right corner of the interface, you'll also see your available credits. Each video you generate will consume a certain number of credits depending on the model and resolution.

### Step 4: Select Video Resolution
On the bottom-left corner, you can choose the video resolution. For example, select **9:16** for vertical videos like Instagram Reels, or **16:9** for YouTube-style horizontal videos.

### Step 5: Generate Your Video
Once you've entered your prompt and selected all the desired options, simply press the **Enter** button located at the bottom-right corner. Your AI-generated video will be created in just a few seconds.

</Steps>

<Alert type="info">
Tip: Experiment with different models and resolutions to see what works best for your content.
</Alert>
