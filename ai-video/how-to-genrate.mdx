# How to Generate an AI Video

Creating an AI-generated video on Zoice is straightforward. Follow these steps to turn your ideas into a video in just a few clicks.

## Step-by-Step Guide

<Steps>

### Step 1: Navigate to AI Videos
After you log in, you'll find the navigation menu on the left side of the dashboard. Select the **"New Videos"** option. Alternatively, from the homepage, click on **"AI Videos"** to open the video creation interface.

### Step 2: Enter Your Prompt
In the AI Video interface, you'll see a **prompt box** where you can describe the video you want to generate. Be as detailed as possible to get the best results.

### Step 3: Select the Model
In the top-right corner of the prompt box, you'll find a dropdown to choose the model version, such as **Veo 2, Veo 3,** or **Veo 3.1 Fast**. Different models may produce different styles of videos.

### Step 4: Choose the Resolution
On the bottom-left corner, you can select the video resolution. For example, use **16:9** for standard YouTube videos or **9:16** for vertical formats like Instagram Reels.

### Step 5: Generate the Video
After entering the prompt and selecting the model and resolution, press the **Enter** button at the bottom-right corner. Your video will be generated in just a few moments.

</Steps>

<Alert type="info">
Note: The quality and style of the video will depend on the chosen model and the detail of your prompt.
</Alert>
