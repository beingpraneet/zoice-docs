# Video Prompt Guidelines

Creating clear and descriptive prompts is the key to getting high-quality AI videos on Zoice. A well-written prompt helps the AI understand exactly what you want, resulting in better and more accurate video outputs.

## What Is a Prompt?

A *prompt* is a short text description that tells Zoice what kind of video you want to generate. The more specific and detailed your prompt is, the better the results will be.

## Prompt Writing Best Practices

Here are guidelines to help you craft effective prompts for AI video generation:

### Be Clear and Specific
Describe exactly what you want to see in your video. Include details such as:
- **Subject or action** (e.g., “a cat playing piano”)
- **Style or mood** (e.g., “in a cinematic style,” “fun and upbeat”)
- **Background or setting** (e.g., “in a modern living room”)

Good example:
> *Create a cinematic 10-second video of a golden retriever catching a frisbee at sunset on a beach.*

### 🧠 Include Important Attributes
Add elements like:
- **Camera angle** (e.g., “close-up,” “wide shot”)
- **Motion or pacing** (e.g., “slow motion,” “fast cuts”)
- **Color or visual style** (e.g., “vibrant colors,” “black and white”)

### ⏱ Specify Duration
If your video generator supports it, state how long you want the video to be:
> *Generate a 15-second video of a skateboarder performing tricks in a skatepark.*

### 💡 Use Natural Language
Write your prompt as if you’re explaining your idea to another person. Avoid overly short or vague prompts.

### ❌ Avoid Ambiguous Words
Words like *“nice,” “cool,”* or *“interesting”* are too subjective and can lead to unpredictable results.

## Prompt Example Patterns

Below are some example prompt structures you can use:

### 📌 Simple Prompt
> *A calm drone shot of a mountain range at sunrise.*

### 📌 Detailed Prompt
> *A dynamic 20-second video of a blue sports car drifting on a neon city street at night with upbeat electronic music.*

### 📌 Emotion-Focused Prompt
> *A heartwarming scene of a parent hugging a child in a rainy park.*

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

| Mistake | Better Alternative |
|---------|-------------------|
| “Make something cool” | “Generate a 10-second upbeat video of dancers in a neon club” |
| “Animal video” | “A close-up of a panda eating bamboo in a lush forest” |
| “Good sunrise video” | “A peaceful sunrise over the ocean with gentle waves” |

## Prompt and Model Relationship

Different models may interpret prompts differently. If a video doesn’t match your expectation:
- Try rephrasing your prompt
- Add more descriptive details
- Switch to a more advanced model
