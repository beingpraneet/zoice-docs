## Frequently Asked Questions (AI Video)

Find answers to the most common questions users ask about creating AI-generated videos using Zoice.

---

## What type of videos can I generate on Zoice?
Zoice allows you to generate short-form AI videos in various styles, such as cinematic, animated, realistic, or stylized sequences. The final output depends on the model and the detail of your prompt.

## How long does it take to generate a video?
Most videos are generated **within a few seconds**, depending on:
- The model selected (e.g., Veo 2, Veo 3, Veo 3.1 Fast)
- The complexity of the prompt  
- Server activity or high-usage periods

## Why does the video not match my prompt exactly?
AI models interpret prompts based on their training data. If results differ:
- Add more clarity or detail to your prompt
- Specify camera angle, environment, style, and motion
- Try a different model version for better accuracy

## How many credits does one video cost?
Each video consumes a specific number of credits based on:
- The model you choose  
- The resolution (e.g., 16:9, 9:16)
The credit usage for each model is displayed in the top-right corner of the interface.

## Can I choose the video resolution before generating?
Yes. You can select video resolution in the **bottom-left corner** of the interface:
- **16:9** → YouTube, widescreen clips  
- **9:16** → Reels, Shorts, TikTok  
- **1:1** → Square posts  

Different resolutions may slightly affect how the scene is framed.

## Why did my video fail to generate?
A video may fail due to:
- Weak or unclear prompts  
- Temporary server issues  
- Selecting a model that doesn’t support certain features  
- Low account credits  

Try rephrasing your prompt, switching model, or checking credits.

## What is the difference between the available models (e.g., Veo 2, Veo 3, Veo 3.1 Fast)?
- **Veo 2** → Balanced quality and speed  
- **Veo 3** → Higher realism, more detailed outputs  
- **Veo 3.1 Fast** → Faster generation, good for quick iterations  

Choose the model based on whether you want **speed**, **quality**, or a balance of both.

## Can I regenerate or improve a video?
Yes. You can:
- Modify the prompt  
- Change the model  
- Adjust resolution  
and generate a new output. Zoice does not currently support one-click “enhance,” so you must re-generate using updated settings.

## Can I use Zoice videos commercially?
Zoice generally allows commercial usage for generated assets, but always review the **Terms of Use** to confirm usage rights for your account type.

## How can I get better results from my prompts?
Try these tips:
- Add more descriptive details (lighting, style, action, camera angle)
- Avoid vague words like “nice” or “cool”
- Keep prompts structured and meaningful  
- Mention the mood or tone you want  
- Choose an advanced model for higher accuracy

<Alert type="info">
If you’re new to prompting, start with simple descriptions and gradually add details to refine the output.
</Alert>
