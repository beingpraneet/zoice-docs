## Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: December 19, 2025**

At Zoice, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our AI generation platform.

## 1. Information We Collect

### Personal Information
We collect information that identifies you personally:

- **Account Information**: Name, email address, password
- **Billing Information**: Payment method, billing address (processed by Stripe)
- **Profile Information**: Username, profile picture, preferences
- **Communication Data**: Support tickets, emails, feedback

### Usage Information
We automatically collect information about your use of our services:

- **Generation Data**: Prompts, generated content, model selections
- **Technical Data**: IP address, browser type, device information
- **Analytics Data**: Page views, feature usage, session duration
- **Performance Data**: Generation times, error logs, system metrics

### Content You Create
- **Input Prompts**: Text descriptions you provide
- **Reference Files**: Images, videos, audio you upload
- **Generated Output**: Images, videos, voice files created
- **Project Data**: Organization, tags, metadata

### Cookies and Tracking
We use cookies and similar technologies:
- **Essential Cookies**: Required for platform functionality
- **Analytics Cookies**: Track usage and improve services
- **Preference Cookies**: Remember your settings
- **Advertising Cookies**: Deliver relevant ads (opt-out available)

## 2. How We Use Your Information

### Service Delivery
- Process your generation requests
- Deliver generated content
- Manage your account and subscriptions
- Process payments and billing
- Provide customer support

### Service Improvement
- Analyze usage patterns
- Improve AI models and algorithms
- Develop new features
- Fix bugs and optimize performance
- Conduct research and development

### Communication
- Send service updates and notifications
- Respond to inquiries and support requests
- Send marketing communications (opt-out available)
- Notify about account or security issues

### Legal and Security
- Comply with legal obligations
- Enforce our Terms of Service
- Protect against fraud and abuse
- Ensure platform security
- Resolve disputes

## 3. How We Share Your Information

### We Do NOT Sell Your Data
We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

### Service Providers
We share data with trusted service providers:
- **Payment Processing**: Stripe for payment processing
- **Cloud Hosting**: AWS for infrastructure
- **Analytics**: Google Analytics for usage insights
- **Email Services**: SendGrid for transactional emails
- **Support Tools**: Zendesk for customer support

### Legal Requirements
We may disclose information when required by law:
- Court orders or subpoenas
- Law enforcement requests
- Protection of rights and safety
- Fraud prevention
- Compliance with regulations

### Business Transfers
In case of merger, acquisition, or sale:
- Information may be transferred to successor
- You will be notified of any change
- Privacy protections remain in effect

### With Your Consent
We may share information with your explicit consent for specific purposes.

## 4. Data Retention

### Account Data
- Retained while your account is active
- Deleted within 90 days of account closure
- Some data retained for legal compliance

### Generated Content
- Stored per your plan limits
- Automatically deleted after retention period
- Can be manually deleted anytime
- Backups retained for 30 days

### Analytics Data
- Aggregated data retained indefinitely
- Personal identifiers removed after 2 years
- Used for service improvement

## 6. Data Security

### Security Measures
- **Encryption**: Data encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest (AES-256)
- **Access Controls**: Role-based access limitations
- **Authentication**: Multi-factor authentication available
- **Monitoring**: 24/7 security monitoring
- **Audits**: Regular security audits and penetration testing

### Your Responsibility
- Use strong, unique passwords
- Enable two-factor authentication
- Keep credentials confidential
- Report suspicious activity immediately

### Data Breaches
- Prompt notification if breach occurs
- Assistance with protective measures
- Cooperation with authorities
- Transparent communication
