## Sign Up

Follow these steps to create your Zoice account and access all AI tools including video, image, voice, and avatar generation.

## How to Sign Up

<Steps>

### Go to Zoice.com
Open **https://zoice.com** in your browser.  
Click the **Sign Up** button located in the top-right corner of the homepage.

### Enter Your Details
Fill in the required information:

- Your Name  
- Email Address  
- Desired Password  

Click **Register** to create your account.

### Sign Up with Google (Optional)
You can also use **Sign Up with Google** to create your account instantly using your Gmail account.  
No password is required with this method.

### Complete Signup
After submitting the form, your account is created successfully.  
You will be logged in automatically and redirected to the Zoice dashboard.

</Steps>

## Troubleshooting

<Alert type="info">
If you do not receive a verification email or face login issues, try signing up again or use Google login for instant access.
</Alert>
