### Troubleshooting Registration Issues

#### Email already in use
If you see an error that your email is already in use, you might have already created an account. Try [logging in](https://app.zoice.com/login) instead, or reset your password if you’ve forgotten it.

#### Didn't receive verification email
- Check your spam or promotions folder.
- Ensure you entered your email address correctly.
- Wait for a few minutes; sometimes delivery can be delayed.

#### Password requirements not met
Make sure your password meets the minimum security requirements (e.g., length, special characters) mentioned on the form.

#### Still unable to sign up?
If you're facing persistent issues, please contact our support team at [**support@zoice.com**](mailto:support@zoice.com) or try signing up with a Google account for instant access.
