## Overview

Zoice's Voice Generator converts your text into natural, human-like speech. Perfect for voiceovers, audiobooks, podcasts, and more, our AI voice technology delivers high-quality audio with customizable voices and styles.

## Key Features

- **Text-to-Speech**: Convert any text into natural-sounding speech
- **Multiple Voice Options**: Choose from various voices, accents, and languages
- **Emotion Control**: Add emotion and tone to your generated voice
- **High-Quality Audio**: Generate clear, professional-grade audio
- **Fast Processing**: Get your audio files in seconds
- **Custom Pronunciation**: Fine-tune how specific words are pronounced

## How It Works

1. **Enter Your Text**: Type or paste the text you want to convert to speech
2. **Select a Voice**: Choose from available voices and languages
3. **Customize Settings**: Adjust speed, pitch, and emotion
4. **Generate**: Create your audio file
5. **Download**: Save your generated voice audio
