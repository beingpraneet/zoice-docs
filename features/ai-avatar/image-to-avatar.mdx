## Overview

The Image-to-Avatar feature allows you to convert static images into dynamic, animated avatars. Upload a photo and watch as Zoice transforms it into a lifelike digital avatar that can speak, present, and interact.

## How It Works

Image-to-Avatar uses advanced AI to analyze facial features, expressions, and characteristics from your uploaded image, then creates a 3D avatar model that maintains the likeness while adding realistic animations.

## Image Requirements

For best results, your uploaded image should meet these criteria:

### Technical Requirements
- **Format**: JPG, PNG, or WebP
- **Resolution**: Minimum 512x512 pixels, recommended 1024x1024 or higher
- **File Size**: Maximum 10MB
- **Aspect Ratio**: Square or portrait orientation works best

### Content Requirements
- **Face Visibility**: Clear, unobstructed view of the face
- **Lighting**: Well-lit with minimal shadows
- **Angle**: Front-facing or slight angle (avoid extreme side profiles)
- **Expression**: Neutral or slight smile works best
- **Background**: Simple backgrounds produce better results
- **Single Person**: Image should contain only one face

<Warning>
Avoid using images with sunglasses, masks, or anything covering the face, as this may reduce avatar quality.
</Warning>

## Customization Options

### Avatar Settings
- **Animation Style**: Choose from realistic, expressive, or subtle movements
- **Background**: Select or upload custom backgrounds
- **Clothing**: Adjust avatar clothing and appearance
- **Voice**: Add voice narration to your avatar

### Video Settings
- **Resolution**: 720p or 1080p output
- **Duration**: Based on script length (up to 2 minutes)
- **Format**: MP4 video output

## Best Practices

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Photo Selection">
    - Use recent, high-quality photos
    - Ensure good lighting and focus
    - Choose images with neutral backgrounds
    - Front-facing angles work best
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Script Writing">
    - Keep scripts concise and natural
    - Use proper punctuation for pacing
    - Test with short scripts first
    - Match script tone to avatar purpose
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Quality Optimization">
    - Start with high-resolution images
    - Test different photos to find the best result
    - Adjust animation intensity based on use case
    - Preview before final generation
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Tip>
Professional headshots or passport-style photos typically produce the best avatar results.
</Tip>
