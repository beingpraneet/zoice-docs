## Overview

Zoice's AI Avatar feature allows you to create lifelike digital avatars that can speak, present, and engage with your audience. Perfect for video presentations, customer service, education, and content creation.

## Key Features

- **Text-to-Avatar**: Generate avatars from text descriptions
- **Image-to-Avatar**: Create avatars based on uploaded images
- **Realistic Animations**: Natural facial expressions and movements
- **Voice Integration**: Combine with voice generation for talking avatars
- **Customization**: Adjust appearance, clothing, and background
- **Multiple Styles**: Choose from realistic, cartoon, or artistic styles

## How It Works

1. **Choose Creation Method**: Select text-to-avatar or image-to-avatar
2. **Provide Input**: Enter description or upload reference image
3. **Customize Avatar**: Adjust appearance and style settings
4. **Add Voice**: Optionally add voice and script
5. **Generate**: Create your AI avatar
6. **Download**: Save your avatar video or animation

## Avatar Types

### Text-to-Avatar
Create avatars from scratch using text descriptions. Perfect when you have a specific vision in mind.

### Image-to-Avatar
Transform photos or images into animated avatars. Ideal for creating personalized or branded avatars.

<Tip>
For best results with image-to-avatar, use clear, front-facing photos with good lighting.
</Tip>
