## Frequently Asked Questions (AI Avatars)

Here are answers to some of the most common questions users have about generating AI Avatars on Zoice.

---

## What is an AI Avatar?  
An AI Avatar is a digital character created by the Zoice AI using either uploaded images or text prompts. It can be customized and used in videos, branding, and more.

## How do I create an AI Avatar on Zoice?  
To create an AI Avatar, log in to Zoice and go to the **"Avatar Videos"** section. You can either upload a high-quality image or describe your avatar in a text prompt. The AI will generate your avatar based on your input.

## What kind of images should I upload for the best results?  
Use clear, high-resolution images (at least 1024x1024 pixels) with good lighting and a front-facing view. Avoid blurry or dark photos, and keep accessories minimal.

## Can I customize my avatar after it's generated?  
Yes. After generating your avatar, you can customize its appearance by adjusting clothing, background, and other details in the Zoice editor.

## What can I use my AI Avatar for?  
You can use AI Avatars for brand promotion, marketing videos, social media content, and anywhere you want a personalized digital character.

## Are there any limits on how many avatars I can create?  
Zoice may have limits based on your subscription plan or available credits. Check your plan details for more information.

## What if my avatar doesn't look like I expected?  
If your avatar doesn’t match your expectations, try uploading a different image or refining your text prompt. You can also adjust customization settings to get a closer match.
