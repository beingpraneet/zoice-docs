## How to Create an Avatar

With Zoice, you can easily create a custom avatar by either uploading images or using AI prompts. Follow this guide to generate your own personalized avatar.

## Step-by-Step Guide

<Steps>

### Step 1: Access the Avatar Section
Log in to your Zoice account and navigate to the dashboard. On the left side menu, select **"Avatar Videos"**. From there, click on the **"Manage Avatars"** option to proceed.

### Step 2: Create a New Avatar
In the Manage Avatars section, click on the **"Create New"** button. You have two options:

1. **Upload an Image**: If you have an image that you want to use as a base for your avatar, upload it and give your avatar a name.

2. **Generate Using AI Prompts**: If you don’t have an image, you can create an avatar by describing it in the prompt box. Enter the avatar’s name and write a detailed prompt specifying characteristics like skin color, clothing style, background, and more.

### Step 3: Customize Your Avatar
Once you’ve uploaded an image or generated one using a prompt, you can further customize your avatar. Adjust features like clothing, background, and camera angles to get the exact look you want.

### Step 4: Finalize and Save
After customizing, save your avatar. For example, if you’ve created an avatar named “Emily Jones,” you can now use it in videos or for brand promotion.

</Steps>

<Alert type="info">
Tip: Use detailed prompts to get more accurate and personalized avatar images.
</Alert>
