# Introduction to AI Avatars

AI Avatars on Zoice are customizable digital characters that you can generate using either images or AI prompts. These avatars can be used for a variety of purposes, such as brand promotion, marketing, or adding a personal touch to your videos.

## What Are AI Avatars?

AI Avatars are digital personas created by using artificial intelligence. You can either upload an image to base your avatar on, or use text prompts to describe the character you want. Zoice’s AI then generates a fully customized avatar that matches your vision.

## Why Use AI Avatars?

- **Personalization**: Create avatars that represent your brand or personality.
- **Engagement**: Use avatars to add a unique touch to videos, making them more engaging and memorable.
- **Versatility**: Avatars can be used in various contexts, from marketing campaigns to social media content.

## Why Zoice for AI Avatars?

Zoice makes creating AI Avatars simple and intuitive. With Zoice, you get:
- **User-Friendly Interface**: Easily upload images or write prompts in a straightforward interface.
- **High-Quality Output**: The AI generates detailed and realistic avatars that match your specifications.
- **Customization Options**: Fine-tune your avatars with various customization settings for clothing, backgrounds, and more.

## How to Get Started

Creating an AI Avatar on Zoice takes just a few steps:

1. **Choose Your Method**: Decide whether you want to upload an existing image or create an avatar from a text prompt.
2. **Customize Your Avatar**: Use the customization options to adjust the avatar’s appearance.
3. **Generate and Save**: Once you’re happy with the settings, generate your avatar and start using it in your projects.
