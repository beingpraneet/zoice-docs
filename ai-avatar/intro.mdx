# Introduction to AI Avatars

AI Avatars on Zoice are customizable digital characters that you can generate using either images or AI prompts. These avatars can be used for a variety of purposes, such as brand promotion, marketing, or adding a personal touch to your videos.

## What Are AI Avatars?

AI Avatars are digital personas created using artificial intelligence. You can upload an image to base your avatar on, or write a text prompt describing the character you want. Zoice’s AI then generates a fully customized avatar that matches your vision.

## Why Use AI Avatars?

- **Personalization**: Create avatars that represent your brand or personality.
- **Engagement**: Add a unique touch to videos, making them more engaging and memorable.
- **Versatility**: Use avatars in marketing, presentations, social media, and creative projects.

## Why Zoice for AI Avatars?

Zoice makes creating AI Avatars simple and intuitive:

- **User-Friendly Interface**: Easily upload images or write prompts.
- **High-Quality Output**: Generate realistic and detailed avatars.
- **Customization Options**: Fine-tune clothing, background, style, and more.

## How to Get Started

<Steps>

### Choose Your Method
Upload an existing image or describe your desired character using a text prompt.

### Customize Your Avatar
Adjust appearance settings such as clothing, background, and style.

### Generate and Save
Generate your avatar and begin using it in your videos or creative projects.

</Steps>
