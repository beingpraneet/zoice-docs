## Payment Methods

### Accepted Payment Methods
- **Credit/Debit Cards**: Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover
- **Digital Wallets**: PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay
- **Bank Transfer**: For Enterprise plans
- **Cryptocurrency**: Bitcoin, Ethereum (Enterprise only)

### Payment Security
- PCI DSS compliant
- Encrypted transactions
- Secure payment processing via Stripe
- No card details stored on our servers

## Billing Cycle

### Subscription Billing
- Billed monthly on signup date
- Auto-renewal unless cancelled
- Prorated refunds for cancellations
- Credits added immediately upon payment

### Credit Purchases
- One-time payment
- Credits added instantly
- No expiration date
- Receipt emailed immediately

## Managing Your Subscription

### Upgrade/Downgrade Plan
To change your current subscription plan, please [get in touch with our support team](mailto:contact@zoice.com). We will assist you with the transition and ensure any prorated adjustments are correctly applied.

### Cancel Subscription
If you wish to cancel your subscription, please [contact support](mailto:contact@zoice.com). 
- Access continues until the end of the current billing period.
- Any unused credits will remain in your account for future use.

## Refund Policy

### Eligible for Refund
- Unused credit packages within 30 days
- Service outages affecting generation
- Billing errors or duplicate charges
- Subscription cancellation (prorated)

### Not Eligible for Refund
- Used credits
- Subscription fees after 30 days
- Credits purchased more than 30 days ago
- Promotional or bonus credits

### Request Refund
1. Email contact@zoice.com
2. Include order number and reason
3. Response within 2 business days
4. Refund processed within 5-7 business days

## Common Billing Issues

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Payment Declined">
    **Possible Causes:**
    - Insufficient funds
    - Card expired
    - Billing address mismatch
    - Bank security hold
    
    **Solutions:**
    - Verify card details
    - Check with your bank
    - Update billing address
    - Try alternative payment method
    - Contact support for assistance
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Credits Not Added">
    **Possible Causes:**
    - Payment processing delay
    - System sync issue
    - Payment failed
    
    **Solutions:**
    - Wait 5-10 minutes
    - Refresh your dashboard
    - Check payment confirmation email
    - Contact support with order number
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Unexpected Charges">
    **Possible Causes:**
    - Auto-renewal subscription
    - Multiple generations
    - High-cost model usage
    
    **Solutions:**
    - Review usage history
    - Check subscription status
    - Monitor credit consumption
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can't Update Payment Method">
    **Possible Causes:**
    - Browser cache issues
    - Payment processor error
    - Invalid card information
    
    **Solutions:**
    - Clear browser cache
    - Try different browser
    - Verify card details
    - Contact support
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
