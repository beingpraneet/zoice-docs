## Billing Cycle

### Subscription Billing
- Billed monthly on signup date
- Auto-renewal unless cancelled
- Prorated refunds for cancellations
- Credits added immediately upon payment

### Credit Purchases
- One-time payment
- Credits added instantly
- No expiration date
- Receipt emailed immediately

## Managing Your Subscription

### View Billing Information
1. Go to **Settings** > **Billing**
2. View current plan and credit balance
3. See usage history and invoices
4. Download receipts

### Upgrade/Downgrade Plan
1. Navigate to **Settings** > **Billing**
2. Click **Change Plan**
3. Select new plan
4. Confirm changes
5. Prorated adjustment applied

### Cancel Subscription
1. Go to **Settings** > **Billing**
2. Click **Cancel Subscription**
3. Confirm cancellation
4. Access continues until end of billing period
5. Unused credits remain in account

## Invoices & Receipts

### Accessing Invoices
- Available in **Settings** > **Billing** > **Invoices**
- Downloadable as PDF
- Emailed automatically after each payment
- Includes detailed usage breakdown

### Invoice Information
- Invoice number and date
- Billing period
- Credits purchased/used
- Amount paid
- Payment method
- Tax information (if applicable)

## Refund Policy

### Eligible for Refund
- Unused credit packages within 30 days
- Service outages affecting generation
- Billing errors or duplicate charges
- Subscription cancellation (prorated)

### Not Eligible for Refund
- Used credits
- Subscription fees after 30 days
- Credits purchased more than 30 days ago
- Promotional or bonus credits

### Request Refund
1. Email contact@zoice.com
2. Include order number and reason
3. Response within 2 business days
4. Refund processed within 5-7 business days

## Common Billing Issues

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Payment Declined">
    **Possible Causes:**
    - Insufficient funds
    - Card expired
    - Billing address mismatch
    - Bank security hold
    
    **Solutions:**
    - Verify card details
    - Check with your bank
    - Update billing address
    - Try alternative payment method
    - Contact support for assistance
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Credits Not Added">
    **Possible Causes:**
    - Payment processing delay
    - System sync issue
    - Payment failed
    
    **Solutions:**
    - Wait 5-10 minutes
    - Refresh your dashboard
    - Check payment confirmation email
    - Contact support with order number
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Unexpected Charges">
    **Possible Causes:**
    - Auto-renewal subscription
    - Multiple generations
    - High-cost model usage
    
    **Solutions:**
    - Review usage history
    - Check subscription status
    - Monitor credit consumption
    - Set usage alerts
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Can't Update Payment Method">
    **Possible Causes:**
    - Browser cache issues
    - Payment processor error
    - Invalid card information
    
    **Solutions:**
    - Clear browser cache
    - Try different browser
    - Verify card details
    - Contact support
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Tax Information

### Sales Tax
- Applied based on billing address
- Varies by location
- Shown before payment confirmation
- Included in invoices

### VAT (European Union)
- VAT charged for EU customers
- Reverse charge for businesses with valid VAT ID
- Enter VAT ID in billing settings

### Business Information
- Add company name and tax ID
- Update in **Settings** > **Billing** > **Business Info**
- Appears on all invoices
