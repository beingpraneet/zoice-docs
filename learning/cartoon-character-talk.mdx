A talking cartoon character video lets you bring any illustrated or animated character to life using artificial intelligence. You no longer need complex animation software or expensive studios to create engaging cartoon videos.

With Zoice, you can upload a cartoon character image, create an AI avatar, add a script or voice, and generate a realistic talking video with accurate lip synchronization and expressive facial animations.

Whether you are an educator, content creator, marketer, or storyteller, AI cartoon avatars make it easy to produce unique videos for YouTube, social media, educational content, and entertainment.

In this article, you will learn how to make a cartoon character talk using AI with Zoice by following a complete step by step process.

## What is a Talking Cartoon Character AI Avatar?

A talking cartoon character AI avatar is a digital animated character that speaks and moves using artificial intelligence. Instead of manually animating a character frame by frame, AI analyzes your uploaded cartoon image and generates realistic lip movements, facial expressions, and head motions synchronized with your script or audio.

Talking cartoon avatars are widely used for:

- YouTube animations and educational content
- Brand mascot videos
- Children's storytelling
- Social media entertainment
- Marketing campaigns
- Online courses

## How to Make a Cartoon Character Talk Using AI

Creating a talking cartoon character video with Zoice only takes a few minutes. First, create or choose an AI avatar using your cartoon image, add your script or audio, customize the output settings, and generate your video. Follow the detailed steps below.

### Step 1: Visit Zoice and Click Sign In

Go to the [Zoice website](https://zoice.com) and click the **Sign In** button at the top-right corner of the homepage. This will open the login page where you can access your Zoice account.

![Zoice Homepage](../../images/homepage.png)

### Step 2: Create a New Zoice Account

If you are using Zoice for the first time, click **Sign Up** to create a new account.

![Sign Up](../../images/sign-up.png)

Enter the required details:

- Name
- Email address
- Password

You can also select **Sign up with Google** to quickly create your account.

### Step 3: Sign In to Your Zoice Account

After creating your account, return to the sign-in page and log in using your registered email and password. You can also choose **Sign in with Google** if your account is connected with Google. Once signed in, you will be redirected to the Zoice dashboard.

![Sign In](../../images/sign-in.png)

### Step 4: Navigate to Avatar Characters

After logging in, you will reach the Zoice dashboard. From the left sidebar, click **Avatar Characters** under the Manage section.

![Avatar Characters Dashboard](../../images/avatar-characters.png)

This is where you can create and manage custom AI avatars using your cartoon character image or any other image.

### Step 5: Click Create New Avatar

On the Avatar Characters page, click the **Create New** button to start creating your custom cartoon avatar.

![Create New Avatar](../../images/ca-4.png)

This will open the avatar creation page where you can either upload an existing cartoon image or generate a new avatar image using an AI prompt.

### Step 6: Upload Your Cartoon Image and Name Your Avatar

Upload a clear, high-quality cartoon character image that you want to bring to life.

![Upload Cartoon Image](../../images/ca-5.png)

Add an **Cartoon Avatar Name** to easily identify your avatar later, then click **Generate Avatar** to create your custom AI cartoon avatar.

For better results, use an image with:

- Clear face visibility and forward-facing angle
- Good contrast between character and background
- Closed or neutral mouth position
- No blurry or low-quality images

    * **Upload Your Image or Generate an Avatar Using AI Prompt**

    Zoice gives you two options to create your cartoon avatar:

    - **Upload Image:** Upload a clear cartoon character image directly from your device.
    - **AI Prompt:** If you don't have an image, select the **AI Prompt** option and describe the cartoon character you want to create. Zoice will generate a custom AI image based on your prompt.

    ![AI Prompt Option](../../images/ca-51.png)

    * We use to upload the image of lion.*

    ![Upload Image](../../images/cartoon-loin-talk.png)

After adding your image, enter a unique avatar name and click **Generate Avatar**.

### Step 7: Select Your Created Cartoon Avatar

After the avatar is generated and saved, go back to the Avatar Characters list. Locate your newly created cartoon avatar and select it to use it for your talking video.

![Select Cartoon Avatar](../../images/pet-select-cartoon.png)

### Step 8: Click Generate Video

With your cartoon avatar selected, click the **Generate Video** button to open the video generation settings panel.

![Generate Video](../../images/pet-generate-video.png)

### Step 9: Add Your Script 

In the video generation panel, Click on script box the popup winodw will apper here we write the detailed script also select the voide model and audio tag like emotion accent, delivry and then click on Add Script.

![Add Script](../../images/add-your-script.png)

### Step 10: Click on Select Voice

After adding the script, Click on Select Voice option, and you will see few public voice and also you can upload your own voice for cartoon avatar.

![Select Voice](../../images/pet-select-voice.png)

### Step 11: Choose a Voice for Your Cartoon Avatar

Browse through the available voices and click on the one that matches your character's style. You can preview each voice before selecting it to make sure it fits your cartoon character's personality.

![Choose a Voice](../../images/pet-choose-voice.png)

### Step 12: Choose Output Settings

After selecting a voice, customize the video output settings based on your platform needs:

- **Aspect Ratio:** 16:9 for YouTube, 9:16 for TikTok or Instagram Reels, 1:1 for social media posts
- **Resolution:** Up to 4K for high-quality output

![Update Output Settings](../../images/pet-update-output-settings.png)

### Step 13: Add an Action Prompt

Click on **Action Prompt** to set the expression and animated actions of your cartoon character. You can choose from options like:

- Happy and expressive
- Excited and energetic
- Calm and professional
- Smiling and friendly

Select or write a custom action prompt that matches the mood and tone of your video content.

![Action Prompt](../../images/pet-action-prompt.png)

### Step 14: Generate Your Talking Cartoon Avatar Video

Once all your settings are ready, click the **Generate** button. Zoice will process your script and cartoon avatar to create the talking video.

![Generate Video](../../images/pet-generate-cartoon-video.png)

### Step 15: Preview the Audio and Generate the Video

Before generating the final video, Zoice creates an audio preview. Listen carefully to check:

- Voice quality and clarity
- Timing and pacing of the speech
- Overall tone and expression

![Generated Audio](../../images/pet-generated-audio.png)

If everything sounds good, click **Generate Avatar Video** to create your final talking cartoon video.

### Step 16: Wait for Your Cartoon Avatar Video to Process

Zoice will now process and render your talking cartoon avatar video. Processing time may vary depending on the length of your script and selected resolution. You can stay on the page or return later to check on the progress.

### Step 17: Preview and Download Your Talking Cartoon Avatar Video

Once your video is ready, preview it to confirm the lip-sync quality, voice match, and overall output.

![Download Video](../../images/pet-download-video.png)
