## What Does "Make a Cartoon Character Talk" Mean?

With Zoice, you can take any cartoon or illustrated character image and bring it to life — giving it a realistic voice, expressive lip-sync, and animated facial movements. This is perfect for storytelling, educational content, YouTube animations, and branded mascot videos.

## Prerequisites

Before starting, ensure you have:
- A clear, front-facing cartoon character image (PNG or JPG).
- A script or pre-recorded audio you want the character to speak.

---

## Step-by-Step Guide

<Steps>
<Step title="Navigate to AI Avatar Videos">
Log in to your [Zoice Dashboard](https://zoice.com) and click **See all tools**. Scroll down and locate **AI Avatar Videos**, then click **Generate**.
<Frame>
![Select AI Avatar Videos](../../images/ca-2.png)
</Frame>
</Step>

<Step title="Upload Your Cartoon Character Image">
Click on **Custom Avatar** and then **Upload Image**. Select your cartoon character file. Make sure the face is forward-facing and the mouth is closed for best lip-sync results.
<Frame>
![Upload Cartoon Character](../../images/custom-avatar-gallery.png)
</Frame>
</Step>

<Step title="Add Your Script or Audio">
Enter your script in the text field, or switch to **Audio Upload** to use a pre-recorded voiceover. Choose a voice from the Zoice voice library to match your character's personality.
<Frame>
![Add Script or Audio](../../images/add-your-script.png)
</Frame>
</Step>

<Step title="Configure Animation Settings">
Set the **Expression Intensity** to match the energy of your character — use **Expressive** for lively cartoon characters. Choose your video aspect ratio based on your target platform (16:9 for YouTube, 9:16 for Shorts/TikTok).
<Frame>
![Configure Settings](../../images/ca-11.png)
</Frame>
</Step>

<Step title="Generate and Download">
Click **Generate**. Once processing completes, preview your animated cartoon character video. Download it or share directly from the dashboard.
<Frame>
![Generate Video](../../images/generate-video.png)
</Frame>
</Step>
</Steps>

## Best Practices for Cartoon Characters

<AccordionGroup>
<Accordion title="Image Quality Tips">
- **High Resolution**: Use at least 512×512 pixel images for sharp animation results.
- **Clear Face**: Ensure the cartoon face is unobstructed, well-lit, and centered in the frame.
- **Closed Mouth**: Starting with a closed-mouth image produces the most natural lip movements.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Voice Matching">
- Choose a voice tone that matches your character's personality (e.g., playful for kids' content, authoritative for educational videos).
- Use **Voice Cloning** if you want a unique voice tailored specifically to your character.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Expression Settings">
- Cartoon characters often benefit from **High** or **Expressive** intensity settings.
- Match the emotion preset (Happy, Excited, Neutral) to the tone of your script.
</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
