Creating a video presence for your brand used to mean booking studio time, hiring a presenter, and spending hours editing. With Zoice Avatar X, you can do it all from a single photo and a short voice recording — no camera, no crew, no retakes.

Zoice Avatar X is Zoice's proprietary AI model that powers the Digital Twin experience. It analyzes your photo and voice sample to build a hyper-realistic digital version of you that can deliver any script on camera with natural facial expressions, lip-syncing, and voice matching.

Once your Digital Twin is created, you can generate unlimited videos — in 100+ languages — with just a typed script. Your avatar looks like you, sounds like you, and performs like you, every single time.

In this guide, you will learn exactly how to create your first Digital Twin video avatar using Zoice Avatar X, from uploading your photo to downloading your finished AI video.


## What is Zoice Avatar X?

Zoice Avatar X is the AI engine behind Zoice's Digital Twin technology. It combines advanced facial animation, voice cloning, and lip-syncing into a single, unified model. Unlike traditional AI avatars that rely on generic stock faces, Avatar X is trained directly on **your** photo and voice, producing a digital presenter that is uniquely yours.

**Zoice Avatar X can:**
- Capture and replicate your facial structure, skin tone, and appearance from a single photo
- Clone your voice using a short audio sample, preserving tone, accent, and natural speech rhythm
- Lip-sync your avatar to any script with frame-accurate mouth movements
- Deliver content in 100+ languages while maintaining your voice characteristics
- Produce videos in up to 4K resolution with natural-looking head movements and expressions

> **The key difference:** A regular AI avatar uses a stock character. Your Digital Twin uses **you** as the source — making every video feel like you genuinely recorded it.

## How to Create Your Digital Twin in 4 Steps

Creating your Digital Twin with Zoice Avatar X takes less than 10 minutes. Here's the complete process:

### Step 1: Visit Zoice.com and Sign In

Go to [Zoice.com](https://zoice.com) in your web browser and click the **Sign In** button in the top-right corner. Enter your email and password, or use **Sign in with Google** to access your account instantly.

![Zoice Homepage](/images/signin.gif)

If you don't have an account yet, click **Sign Up**, fill in your name, email, and password, and you'll be on the dashboard in under a minute.

### Step 2: Create Your Avatar Profile

From the Zoice dashboard, click **Avatar Characters** in the left sidebar, then click **Create New** to start building your Digital Twin. Upload a clear, front-facing photo of yourself, give your avatar a name, and click **Generate Avatar**. Zoice Avatar X will process your image and create your animated avatar profile in moments.

Next, set up your voice profile by going to the **Voices** section. Click **Create Voice Profile**, upload or record a short audio sample of yourself speaking naturally, and save your profile. This is the voice your Digital Twin will use in every video you generate.

![Create Avatar and Voice Profile](/images/create-your-avatar-profile.gif)

<Tip>
**Photo tips:** Use a front-facing photo with clear, even lighting and a simple background. A neutral or slightly smiling expression gives Avatar X the most flexibility to animate.

**Voice tips:** Record in a quiet room without background noise or echo. Speaking naturally for at least 30 seconds gives the AI enough material to produce a high-quality voice clone.
</Tip>

### Step 3: Configure Your Avatar Settings

After your avatar profile is created, click **Avatar Setting** to add key details that help Avatar X produce more accurate results:

- **Avatar Name:** A recognizable label for this profile
- **Gender:** Male or Female
- **Age:** Young Adult, Adult, or Senior
- **Ethnicity:** Helps Avatar X calibrate facial expression rendering

![Avatar Settings](/images/digital-twin-avatar-settings.png)

Once saved, your Digital Twin avatar profile is ready to use.

### Step 4: Select Your Digital Twin and Generate Video

From the **Avatar Characters** library, click on your Digital Twin avatar to select it, then click **Generate Video** to open the video creation workspace.

![Select Avatar and Generate Video](/images/digital-twin-generate-video.png)

### Step 5: Add Your Script

In the video creation workspace, click on the **Script Box**. Type or paste the text you want your Digital Twin to deliver, then select your cloned voice from the dropdown and click **Add Script**.

![Script Box](/images/digital-twin-script-box.png)

![Add Script](/images/digital-twin-add-script.png)

<Tip>
Write your script the way you naturally speak. Short, clear sentences work better than long, complex ones. For multilingual videos, paste the translated script and your Digital Twin will deliver it in that language while keeping your cloned voice.
</Tip>

### Step 6: Select Your Cloned Voice

Click the **Select Voice** button and choose your cloned voice profile. Preview it to confirm it sounds right, then confirm your selection.

![Select Voice](/images/digital-twin-choose-voice.png)

### Step 7: Configure Output Settings

Click **Output Settings** to choose the video format:

- **Aspect Ratio:** 16:9 for YouTube, website, or LinkedIn, or 9:16 for Instagram Reels, TikTok, or YouTube Shorts
- **Resolution:** Up to 4K for maximum clarity

![Output Settings](/images/digital-twin-output-settings.png)

### Step 8: Add an Action Prompt (Optional)

Click **Action Prompt** to control how your Digital Twin behaves during the video. You can write a custom prompt or select from suggested styles. For example:

*"Speak with a calm and confident tone. Maintain eye contact with the camera. Use natural, subtle head nods to emphasize key points."*

![Click Action Prompt](/images/digital-twin-click-action-prompt.png)

![Action Prompt](/images/digital-twin-action-prompt.png)

### Step 9: Generate Your Digital Twin Video

Once your script, voice, output settings, and action prompt are all set, click the **Generate** button to start the Avatar X rendering process.

![Generate Video](/images/digital-twin-generate.png)

### Step 10: Preview the Audio

Before the final video renders, Zoice creates an audio preview so you can verify the voice, pronunciation, and pacing. Listen carefully and confirm everything sounds right, then click **Generate Avatar Video**.

![Audio Preview](/images/digital-twin-audio-preview.png)

### Step 11: Preview and Download Your Digital Twin Video

Once the video finishes processing, play the result to review the avatar performance, lip-sync accuracy, and overall video quality.

![Download Video](/images/digital-twin-download.png)

If you're satisfied, click **Download** to save the video to your device. Your Digital Twin video is now ready to publish on YouTube, social media, your website, or anywhere else.

## Tips for Getting the Best Results from Avatar X

### Photo Quality Tips

Getting the best photo is the single biggest factor in Digital Twin quality. Keep these in mind:

- **Use a well-lit, front-facing photo.** Avoid sunglasses, heavy shadows across the face, or extreme side angles.
- **Keep the background clean and simple.** A plain wall or neutral background helps Avatar X isolate your face cleanly.
- **Use a recent photo.** Avatar X replicates your current appearance, so use a photo that reflects how you look today.
- **Higher resolution = better output.** A sharp, high-resolution image produces smoother, more realistic animations.
- **Neutral expression works best.** A relaxed, neutral, or slightly smiling expression gives the AI more flexibility to animate a wider range of emotions.

### Voice Recording Tips

- **Record in a quiet room.** Even mild background noise or room echo reduces voice cloning quality.
- **Speak at your normal pace.** Don't rush or slow down unnaturally. The AI clones your natural rhythm.
- **Use at least 30 seconds of audio.** The more clean audio you provide, the better the voice clone.
- **Avoid music or sound effects** in your sample recording.

### Script Writing Tips

- **Write conversationally.** Your Digital Twin performs best with natural, spoken-style language, not dense, formal writing.
- **Use punctuation to control pacing.** Commas create natural pauses. Periods signal the end of a thought. Use them intentionally.
- **Keep sentences short.** Long sentences can sound robotic. Break them into smaller, more natural chunks.
- **Test with a short script first.** Before generating a long video, generate a 30-second test to check that the voice and avatar match your expectations.


## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I create multiple Digital Twins?**
Yes. There is no hard limit on the number of avatar character profiles you can create, as long as you have available credits.

**How many voice profiles can I create?**
The number of voice profiles depends on your subscription plan. Check your plan details for specifics.

**Can I upload audio instead of typing a script?**
Yes. In the video creation workspace, you can switch to **Generate from Audio** mode and upload an MP3, WAV, or M4A file directly, instead of typing a script.

**Can I use my Digital Twin in a different language?**
Absolutely. Zoice Avatar X supports 100+ languages. Simply type or paste your script in the target language and select a voice in that language. Your avatar will deliver the content while preserving your visual likeness.

**Can I import voices from other platforms like ElevenLabs?**
Yes. You can import voice profiles from ElevenLabs by entering your API key in your Zoice voice settings.

**How long does it take to generate a Digital Twin video?**
Most videos are processed within a few minutes, depending on script length and current server load.

**What image formats are supported?**
Zoice accepts JPG, JPEG, and PNG image formats for avatar creation.
