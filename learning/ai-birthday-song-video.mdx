## What is an AI Birthday Video Card?

An AI birthday video card is a customized animation where a static portrait is brought to life using vocal audio. Rather than requiring expensive video editing, the AI program processes the audio waves and maps them onto the subject's lips, jawline, and facial muscles.

The person in the photo appears to sing or speak the birthday greeting naturally, making it a perfect surprise to share on messaging apps and social media feeds.

## How to Make AI Birthday Song Video with Photo

Follow these detailed steps to compile and render your personalized birthday greeting.

### Step 1: Open the Zoice Platform

Visit the main website at [Zoice.com](https://zoice.com) to access the AI generation dashboard.

![Zoice Homepage](/images/homepage.png)

### Step 2: Sign In to Your Dashboard

Click the **Sign In** button at the top of the screen. Log in using your email and password, or authenticate instantly using your Google profile.

![Sign In](/images/sign-in.png)

### Step 3: Create a New Profile (For New Users)

If you are a first-time visitor, select **Sign Up** to create your free account. Fill in your name, email, and password to initialize your workspace.

![Sign Up](/images/sign-up.png)

### Step 4: Open the Avatar Library

From your dashboard, click on **Avatar Characters**. Choose an existing presenter card from your collection, or click **Create New** to upload a new birthday image.

![Avatar Character](/images/avatar-characters.png)

    * **Click on Create New**

![Create New](/images/ca-4.png)

### Step 5: Upload the Celebrant's Photo

Select **Upload a Photo** and select the picture of the birthday person from your local directory. Name the character (e.g., "Birthday Boy John").

![Upload Photo](/images/ca-5.png)

Ensure the photo is clear and has a front-facing view of the person's face. We uploaded this sample image:

![Used Image](/images/birthday-photo-uploaded.png)

Once uploaded, the character card will be saved to your dashboard library:

![Avatar Preview](/images/birthday-photo-preview.png)

*Note: You can also generate a custom party character using the **AI Prompt** toggle by writing descriptive text (e.g., "A happy cartoon mascot wearing a party hat, 3D style").*

### Step 6: Configure Profile Details

Click the **Avatar settings** tab next to **Generate video** to open the customization window. Under the **General** tab, you can add and refine details such as Name, Gender, Age, and Ethnicity. Once configured, click the **Save** button in the bottom-right corner.

![Avatar Settings Option](/images/birthday-photo-avatar-settings-option.png)

* **Fill in avatar settings gerneral information**

![Avatar Settings Modal](/images/birthday-photo-avatar-settings.png)

### Step 7: Select Generate Video Option

With your newly created profile selected, click the **Generate Video** button to open the editor timeline.

![Generate Video](/images/birthday-photo-generate-video-option.png)

### Step 8: Choose Generate from Audio Option

Toggle the dropdown menu from **Generate from script** to **Generate from audio**.

* **Select Generate from Audio**

![Generate from Audio](/images/birthday-photo-generate-from-audio.png)

### Step 9: Upload Your Birthday Song Track

Click the **Upload Audio** box (indicated by the yellow arrow) to upload or drag-and-drop your birthday song file (MP3, WAV, or M4A format) from your local device.

![Upload Song](/images/birthday-photo-upload-audio.png)

### Step 10: Playback Your Audio

Once the track is successfully uploaded, you will see the file name inside the box (indicated by the yellow arrow). Click the **Play** button on the left of the timeline to preview your audio and ensure it plays correctly.

![Audio Preview](/images/birthday-photo-audio-preview.png)

### Step 11: Set Video Aspect Ratio

Select the **9:16 ratio** for a standard portrait video format, suitable for mobile cards (TikTok, WhatsApp Status, Instagram Reels).

![Aspect Ratio Settings](/images/birthday-photo-update-output-settings.png)

### Step 12: Add an Action Prompt

Click the **Action Prompt** button to choose how the birthday subject behaves while performing the song.

![Action Prompt](/images/birthday-photo-action-prompt.png)

### Step 13: Write a Happy Performance Instruction

Input a description of the desired behavior (e.g., *"Cheerful facial expressions, big smile, laughing, nodding happily in a party setting"*).

![Action Prompt Selection](/images/birthday-photo-action-prompt-box.png)

### Step 14: Click Generate to Begin

Review your settings and click the **Generate** button to send your task to the processing queue.

![Singing Video Generation](/images/birthday-photo-generate.png)

### Step 15: Confirm the Audio Preview

Listen to the audio preview to check the vocal sync. If everything sounds perfect, click **Generate Avatar Video** to render the final animation.

![Generated Audio Preview](/images/birthday-photo-generated-audio.png)

### Step 16: Download the Birthday Video

Once processing is complete, click **Download** to save the MP4 video to your device. It is now ready to send via chat, email, or post on social channels.

![Download Video](/images/birthday-photo-download-video.png)

> [!NOTE]
> **Adding Background Music**: Zoice generates the lip-synced vocal performance video without background music tracks. To add background music or instrumental tracks to your birthday video, open any video editor (such as CapCut, Premiere Pro, or Canva), import your generated Zoice avatar video, and add your music track as an additional audio layer.

## Tips for Creating Better AI Birthday Song Videos

To get the highest quality results when you make ai birthday song video with photo, apply these creation tips:
* **Choose Happy Portraits**: Select clear, front-facing images of your friend or family member with cheerful facial lighting so the lip sync matches their expression.
* **Customize Action Prompts**: Add expressive terms like "wide smile", "laughing eyes", or "winking dynamically" to make the birthday greeting feel festive.
* **Select Upbeat Songs**: Pick high-quality birthday vocal tracks or recordings, and toggle the Enhance Audio feature for clear sound.
