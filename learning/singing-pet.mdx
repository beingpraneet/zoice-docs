Creating fun, viral content has never been easier thanks to artificial intelligence. Instead of just posting cute photos of animals, you can now bring them to life by making them sing to your favorite audio tracks. 

With Zoice, you can upload an image of a dog, cat, or any other pet, upload a vocal track or song, and generate an incredibly realistic singing pet video complete with natural head movements and lip-syncing.

Whether you are creating viral TikToks, entertaining YouTube Shorts, or funny memes to share with friends, singing pet avatars are a great way to capture attention and make people smile.

In this article, you will learn how to create a singing pet avatar video using Zoice by following a comprehensive, step-by-step tutorial.

## What is a Singing Pet Avatar?

A singing pet avatar is a digital animation created from a static image of a pet, which is then synchronized to an audio track using artificial intelligence. The AI analyzes the audio file and animates the pet's mouth, eyes, and head to match the rhythm, vocals, and emotion of the sound.

This technology allows you to create highly engaging and often humorous videos without needing 3D animation skills or complex video editing software. You just need a picture and a song.

## How to Make a Singing Pet Avatar Video Using AI

Creating a singing pet video with Zoice takes only a few minutes. To begin, create or choose your pet avatar, upload your audio track, configure the output settings, and generate your video. Follow the detailed steps below to get started.

### Step 1: Visit Zoice.com

Go to [Zoice.com](https://zoice.com) in your web browser. This is the main portal where you can access all of Zoice's AI creation tools.

![Zoice Homepage](/images/homepage.png)

### Step 2: Sign In to Your Zoice Account

Click the **Sign In** button in the top-right corner of the homepage. Enter your registered email address and password, or choose **Sign in with Google** to access your dashboard quickly.

![Sign In](/images/sign-in.png)

### Step 3: Create a New Zoice Account (For New Users)

If you don't have a Zoice account yet, click **Sign Up** on the login page. Fill in your details (name, email, and password) or select **Sign up with Google** to create a new account in one click.

![Sign Up](/images/sign-up.png)

### Step 4: Click Avatar Character

Select **Avatar Character** to begin creating a new AI pet avatar. If you already have a pet avatar saved, you can choose it from your library and proceed to the next step.

![Avatar Character](/images/avatar-characters.png)

    * **Click on Create New**

![Create New](/images/ca-4.png)

### Step 5: Upload or Generate Avatar Image

Now, You have two options to set up your AI singing pet avatar:

* **Upload a Photo**: Upload a clear portrait image of your pet or an animal from your computer and give your singer a name.

![Upload Photo](/images/ca-5.png)

A high-quality, front-facing image where the pet's mouth is visible helps Zoice generate more natural facial animations and accurate lip-syncing. We have uploaded an HD image and created a pet avatar, as shown below:

![Avatar Preview](/images/singing-pet-avatar-preview.png) 

* **Use an AI Prompt**: Toggle the **AI Prompt** option.

![AI Prompt](/images/ca-51.png)

Enter a descriptive text prompt explaining what your pet avatar should look like (e.g., *"A cute golden retriever wearing sunglasses in a recording studio"*). Click **Generate Image**, review the generated image, name your avatar, and save it to your library.

### Step 6: Configure Avatar Settings (Optional)

After adding the image to your library, click **Avatar Setting** to add basic details such as Name, Gender, and Age (if applicable). Providing these details can help organize your avatar library. Once done, save and close the avatar settings window.

![Avatar Settings](/images/singing-pet-avatar-settings.png)

### Step 7: Click Generate Video

With your pet avatar profile ready, click **Generate Video** to open the video editor and add your singing audio.

![Generate Video](/images/singing-pet-generate-video.png)

### Step 8: Select Generate from Audio

Switch the input mode from **Generate from Script** to **Generate from Audio** to create a singing avatar using a sound file. You can upload a pre-recorded audio file such as a famous song, a funny voiceover, or an original music track.

![Generate from Audio](/images/singing-pet-generate-from-audio.png)

### Step 9: Upload the Song

Upload the audio file (MP3, WAV, or M4A format) that you want your pet avatar to sing. You can select a file from your device or choose a trending song from Zoice's built-in library.

![Upload Song](/images/singing-pet-upload-song.png)

### Step 10: Preview Your Uploaded Audio

Once the audio is uploaded, click **Preview** to listen to the track and confirm that the audio has loaded correctly and plays from the right starting point.

![Preview Audio](/images/singing-pet-preview-audio.png)

### Step 11: Update Output Settings

Configure the output settings to suit your target platform:

* **Aspect Ratio**: Choose the aspect ratio matching your target platform (e.g., 9:16 for portrait/TikTok/Shorts, 16:9 for landscape/YouTube).
* **Resolution**: Set the resolution up to 4K for maximum clarity.

![Update Output Settings](/images/singing-pet-update-output-settings.png)

### Step 12: Configure the Action Prompt

Click **Action Prompt** to define the facial expressions and physical gestures of your singing pet avatar during the video.

![Action Prompt](/images/singing-pet-action-prompt.png)

### Step 13: Select or Write an Action Prompt

Write a custom prompt to specify your pet avatar's expressions (e.g., *"The dog sings enthusiastically, bobbing its head to the beat with energetic expressions"*).

![Action Prompt Selection](/images/singing-pet-action-prompt-box.png)

### Step 14: Generate Your Singing Avatar Video

Once all settings are configured, click the **Generate** button to begin the generation process.

![Singing Video Generation](/images/singing-pet-generate.png)

### Step 15: Preview the Generated Audio

Before processing the final video, Zoice generates an audio preview. Listen to the preview to verify the audio quality and timing. 

If the preview sounds correct, click **Generate Avatar Video** to render the final video.

![Generated Audio Preview](/images/singing-pet-generated-audio.png)

**Tip**: You can toggle the **Enhance Audio** switch in the top-right corner of the script box to automatically improve the sound quality of the uploaded track before rendering.

### Step 15: Download Your Singing AI Avatar Video

Once the rendering is complete and you are satisfied with the result, click the **Download** button to save the singing pet video to your device. It is now ready to share on social media or send to friends.

![Download Video](/images/download-singing-pet-video.png)
