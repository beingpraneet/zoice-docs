## Why Use AI Avatars in Healthcare Marketing?

Healthcare organizations need to communicate clearly, build trust, and reach diverse audiences — all while managing tight content budgets. AI avatars created with Zoice let you produce professional presenter-led videos at scale, without the need for on-camera talent. Use them for patient education, product explainers, clinic promotions, and more.

## Prerequisites

Before starting, ensure you have:
- A professional headshot or stock avatar suited to the healthcare context.
- A clear, factual script reviewed for medical accuracy.
- Optional: a branded logo or background image for your clinic/company.

## Step-by-Step Guide

<Steps>
<Step title="Navigate to AI Avatar Videos">
Log in to your [Zoice Dashboard](https://zoice.com) and click **See all tools**. Scroll down and locate **AI Avatar Videos**, then click **Generate**.
<Frame>
![Select AI Avatar Videos](../../images/ca-2.png)
</Frame>
</Step>

<Step title="Choose or Create a Professional Avatar">
Click **Avatar Character** to open the library. For healthcare marketing, choose a **Stock Avatar** that appears professional and approachable, or upload a custom presenter image. A neutral expression, professional attire, and good lighting in the source image builds viewer trust.
<Frame>
![Choose Avatar](../../images/custom-avatar-gallery.png)
</Frame>
</Step>

<Step title="Enter Your Healthcare Script">
Type or paste your healthcare marketing script into the text field. Keep language clear, empathetic, and jargon-free for patient-facing content. Use short sentences and a calm, informative tone.
<Frame>
![Add Script](../../images/add-your-script.png)
</Frame>
</Step>

<Step title="Select a Professional Voice">
In the voice settings, select a voice that sounds warm and authoritative — ideal for healthcare contexts. You can also use **Voice Cloning** to use the voice of a specific spokesperson or doctor with their permission.
<Frame>
![Select Voice](../../images/select-voice-over.png)
</Frame>
</Step>

<Step title="Customize Background & Branding">
Upload a branded background image (clinic interior, branded slide, or neutral gradient). Set the video aspect ratio — 16:9 is standard for presentations and website embeds.
<Frame>
![Configure Video](../../images/ca-11.png)
</Frame>
</Step>

<Step title="Generate and Export">
Review all settings and click **Generate**. Once your video renders, download it in your preferred format for website embedding, social media, or patient portal integration.
<Frame>
![Generate and Export](../../images/generate-video.png)
</Frame>
</Step>
</Steps>

## Best Practices for Healthcare AI Avatar Videos

<AccordionGroup>
<Accordion title="Building Trust with Viewers">
- Use a professional, front-facing avatar with neutral or slightly warm expression.
- Keep the visual environment clean and uncluttered (white or branded backgrounds work best).
- Avoid overly animated or expressive movements — a calm, measured delivery inspires confidence.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Script & Compliance">
- Always have scripts reviewed by a qualified medical professional before publication.
- Include necessary disclaimers (e.g., "Consult your physician before making medical decisions").
- Avoid making specific medical claims unless substantiated by evidence.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Accessibility">
- Enable captions or subtitles for all patient-facing content.
- Choose clear, well-articulated voice profiles so content is accessible for older or hearing-impaired audiences.
</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
