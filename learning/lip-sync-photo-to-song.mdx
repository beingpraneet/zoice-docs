## What is AI Lip-Sync Animation?

AI lip-sync animation is a machine-learning process that animates static pictures based on audio frequencies. The model analyzes vocal files to identify specific sound groups (phonemes) and adjusts corresponding facial points (visemes) on the photo, such as the lip width, jaw position, and eye shape.

This allows you to generate singing animations without needing manual frame manipulation, motion tracking rigs, or complicated 3D animation skills.

## Tips for Creating Better Lip Sync Photo Videos

To ensure high-quality syncing when you create lip sync photo to song animations, follow these configuration practices:
* **High-Resolution Face Mapping**: Use front-facing images with clear lip structures and high lighting contrast so the neural network maps visemes accurately.
* **Control Action Dynamics**: Specify clean head bobbing and blink frequencies in the Action Prompt to prevent distortion.
* **Filter Background Noise**: Ensure your song vocals are isolated or use the Enhance Audio toggle to produce clean lipsync movement.
