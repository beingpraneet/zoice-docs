Photo Avatars are one of the fastest ways to create a realistic, talking AI presenter on Zoice. Instead of recording yourself on video, you simply provide a photo — either by uploading your own image or generating one using an AI text prompt — and Zoice animates it into a fully speaking avatar with natural lip-syncing, expressions, and head movements.

Whether you want to build a personal brand presence, create marketing videos, produce course content, or launch a virtual spokesperson, Photo Avatars give you everything you need from a single image.

This guide walks you through everything: creating your Photo Avatar, writing effective prompts, adding motion, and generating your first video.

## What is a Photo Avatar?

A Photo Avatar is an AI-animated presenter created from a still photograph. Zoice's Avatar X engine analyzes the face in your photo and generates realistic motion, lip-syncing, and expressions to match any script or audio you provide.

Photo Avatars differ from Digital Twins in one key way — a Digital Twin is trained on **your specific photo and voice** to create a personal AI twin, while a Photo Avatar is a broader tool that can animate **any photo** into a talking presenter, making it useful for creating brand characters, fictional spokespeople, or stylized presenters.

**Photo Avatars can be used for:**
- Social media content and YouTube videos
- Product demo and explainer videos
- Online courses and training modules
- News and announcement videos
- Customer support walkthroughs
- Multilingual campaigns in 100+ languages

## Step 1: Sign In to Zoice

Go to [Zoice.com](https://zoice.com) and click **Sign In** in the top-right corner. Enter your email and password, or use **Sign in with Google** for faster access.

![Zoice Homepage](/images/homepage.png)

If you are a new user, click **Sign Up** and fill in your name, email, and password to create a free account.

![Sign Up](/images/sign-up.png)

## Step 2: Go to Avatar Characters

From your Zoice dashboard, click **Avatar Characters** in the left sidebar.

![Avatar Characters](/images/avatar-characters.png)

This is your avatar library. All of your Photo Avatars are created and managed here. Click **Create New** to start creating your first Photo Avatar.

![Create New](/images/ca-4.png)

## Step 3: Create Your Photo Avatar

On the avatar creation page, you have two options to provide a photo:

### Option A: Upload Your Own Photo

Click **Upload Image** and select a photo from your device. This can be a photo of yourself, a professional model, a brand character, or any other person you want to animate.

![Upload Photo](/images/ca-5.png)

<Tip>
**Best practices for uploading photos:**
- Use a clear, front-facing photo with the face fully visible
- Choose an image with even, natural lighting — avoid harsh shadows across the face
- Use a simple or plain background for the cleanest results
- Avoid photos with sunglasses, face coverings, or heavy filters
- Higher resolution images (500×500 pixels or above) produce noticeably better output
- A neutral or gently smiling expression gives Avatar X the most flexibility to animate
</Tip>

### Option B: Generate a Photo Using an AI Prompt

If you don't have a suitable photo or want to create a fictional presenter, toggle the **AI Prompt** option. Type a detailed text description of the avatar you want to create and click **Generate Image**.

![AI Prompt](/images/ca-51.png)

**Example prompts:**
- *"A professional woman in her 30s with short dark hair, wearing a navy blazer, studio portrait lighting, clean white background"*
- *"A friendly male news anchor in his 40s, medium build, wearing a formal grey suit, broadcast studio setting, sharp focus"*
- *"A young female wellness coach with warm skin tone, wearing athletic wear, outdoor park background, natural sunlight"*

Once you are happy with the generated or uploaded image, give your avatar a name and click **Generate Avatar**.

## Step 4: Writing Effective AI Prompts for Photo Avatars

If you're using the AI Prompt option to generate your Photo Avatar, the quality of your prompt directly affects the output. Here's how to write prompts that get great results:

### Structure Your Prompt in Layers

The Avatar X AI model prioritizes the earlier parts of your prompt, so lead with the most important elements first:

1. **Image type and main subject** — describe who the person is
2. **Background and setting** — describe where they appear
3. **Style modifiers** — lighting, tone, mood, and extra details

**Example:**
> *"A portrait photo of a professional female doctor in her 40s wearing a white lab coat, clean hospital background with soft warm lighting, highly detailed, realistic."*

### Prompt Tips

- **Be specific.** Vague prompts produce vague results. The more detail you provide, the more accurate the output.
- **Describe lighting explicitly.** Terms like *"natural lighting," "warm studio lighting,"* or *"soft diffused light"* significantly improve photo realism.
- **Specify image style.** Use terms like *"realistic photo," "cinematic portrait,"* or *"professional headshot"* to set the visual style.
- **Add quality modifiers.** Words like *"high resolution," "sharp focus," "ultrarealistic,"* or *"highly detailed"* help push the output quality higher.
- **Avoid mixing styles.** Stick to one consistent visual style per prompt — mixing *"vintage"* with *"cinematic modern"* can produce inconsistent results.

### Common Prompt Issues and How to Fix Them

| Issue | Fix |
|---|---|
| Output doesn't match the prompt | Use more specific language and restructure — lead with the most important details first |
| Low-quality or blurry image | Add *"high resolution," "sharp focus," "ultrarealistic"* to your prompt |
| Unnatural facial expression | Add *"natural pose," "authentic expression," "relaxed and friendly"* |
| Wrong background | Be more explicit — describe the exact setting, colors, and mood |
| Inconsistent style | Remove conflicting style terms and pick one clear visual direction |
| Bad cropping or composition | Add framing instructions like *"close-up headshot," "mid-range portrait," "wide shot"* |

### Useful Prompt Keywords by Category

| Category | Keywords |
|---|---|
| **Mood** | Confident, warm, professional, friendly, authoritative, calm |
| **Lighting** | Natural lighting, warm studio lighting, soft diffused light, broadcast lighting |
| **Framing** | Close-up, headshot, 3/4 portrait, mid-range shot, wide shot |
| **Quality** | Highly detailed, ultrarealistic, sharp focus, high resolution |
| **Style** | Realistic photo, cinematic portrait, professional headshot |

### Sample Prompts to Get You Started

**Professional presenter:**
> *"A professional photo of a confident male business executive in his 50s, wearing a dark navy suit, modern office background with soft bokeh, warm studio lighting, sharp focus, ultrarealistic."*

**Healthcare avatar:**
> *"A portrait photo of a friendly female doctor in her 30s wearing a white coat and stethoscope, clean clinic setting, warm and approachable expression, natural lighting, highly detailed."*

**News anchor:**
> *"A professional photo of a male news anchor in his 40s in a formal grey suit, broadcast studio background with monitor screens, sharp broadcast lighting, serious and authoritative expression, ultrarealistic."*

## Step 5: Configure Avatar Settings

After your Photo Avatar is created, click **Avatar Setting** to add supporting details that help Avatar X produce better, more personalized animations:

- **Avatar Name** — a label for easy identification
- **Gender** — Male or Female
- **Age** — Young Adult, Adult, or Senior
- **Ethnicity** — helps Avatar X calibrate facial expression rendering and skin tone accuracy

Save these settings before moving on.

## Step 6: Generate Videos With Your Photo Avatar

With your Photo Avatar created, you can now use it to generate talking avatar videos.

From the Avatar Characters library, click on your Photo Avatar to select it, then click **Generate Video** to open the video creation workspace.

### Add Your Script

In the workspace, click the **Script Box** and type or paste the text you want your avatar to speak. Select a voice model and click **Add Script**.

### Select a Voice

Click **Select Voice** to choose from Zoice's library of 50+ public voices — or use your own cloned voice. You can also import voices from ElevenLabs using your API key. Preview voices before confirming your selection.

### Set Output Settings

Choose your video format:
- **16:9** for YouTube, websites, and presentations
- **9:16** for Instagram Reels, TikTok, and YouTube Shorts
- **Resolution** up to 4K

### Add an Action Prompt

Click **Action Prompt** to control how your avatar moves during the video. You can write a custom prompt or select from Zoice's suggested styles:

*"Friendly and professional presenter. Steady eye contact with the camera. Natural head nods at key points. Warm, relaxed expression throughout."*

### Generate and Download

Click **Generate**, preview the audio output, then click **Generate Avatar Video** to render your final video. Once complete, click **Download** to save it to your device.

## Step 7: Adding Motion to Photo Avatars

Zoice lets you enhance your Photo Avatar with different motion settings directly in the video workspace. When you select your Photo Avatar in the editor, click the **Action Prompt** section to adjust the motion engine behavior.

You can control:
- **Head movement intensity** — subtle nods vs. more active movement
- **Facial expression range** — from minimal to highly expressive
- **Eye contact and gaze direction** — maintaining camera focus or natural scanning

**Motion prompting best practices:**

Use **clear, visual descriptions** rather than abstract instructions:

| ❌ Avoid | ✅ Use instead |
|---|---|
| *"Make the avatar look natural"* | *"Subtle head nods, relaxed eye contact, gentle blinking"* |
| *"Don't move too much"* | *"Minimal head movement, steady gaze, calm posture"* |
| *"Make it energetic"* | *"Active hand gestures, expressive eyebrow movement, enthusiastic delivery"* |

Use **positive phrasing** — describe what you want to happen, not what you don't want:

| ❌ Avoid | ✅ Use instead |
|---|---|
| *"No jerky movements"* | *"Smooth and fluid movements throughout"* |
| *"Don't look away from camera"* | *"Consistent, direct eye contact with the camera"* |

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How many Photo Avatars can I create?**
You can create as many Photo Avatar profiles as you need, subject to your subscription credits. Check your plan for specific limits.

**Can I use a Photo Avatar in multiple videos?**
Yes. Once created, your Photo Avatar can be reused across unlimited videos. You don't need to recreate it each time — just select it from your Avatar Characters library.

**What photo formats does Zoice accept?**
Zoice supports JPG, JPEG, and PNG image formats for Photo Avatar creation.

**Can I use a photo of someone other than myself?**
Yes, but you should only use photos of people for whom you have the appropriate rights and permissions to create an avatar.

**Can my Photo Avatar speak in different languages?**
Yes. Zoice supports 100+ languages. Simply type your script in the target language and select a matching voice from the library.

**Can I upload audio instead of typing a script?**
Yes. In the video creation workspace, switch to **Generate from Audio** mode to upload an MP3, WAV, or M4A audio file directly.

**Can I import voices from ElevenLabs?**
Yes. You can connect your ElevenLabs account via API key in your Zoice voice settings to import custom voice profiles.

**My Photo Avatar is not animating correctly — what should I do?**
Make sure you created the avatar through the **Avatar Characters** section and not as a regular image upload. If the issue persists, try using a higher-resolution, front-facing photo with good lighting and a plain background.
