Bringing historical memories to life is one of the most emotional uses of creative artificial intelligence. Instead of keeping vintage family photos locked away in albums, you can now animate them. In this tutorial, you will discover how to make old photo sing a song to create unique digital keepsakes.

By using Zoice’s advanced generation engine, anyone can animate black-and-white portraits, old family snapshots, or classic oil paintings. The AI analyzes your uploaded song or vocal track, maps it onto the facial features of the photo, and renders a realistic singing performance with natural jaw movements and head sways.

Whether you want to create a nostalgic tribute for a family reunion, a historical presentation, or a unique post for social media, learning how to make old photo sing a song is simple, fast, and requires no video editing experience.

## What is a Singing Vintage Photo?

A singing vintage photo is an AI-animated video generated from a static historical portrait. Rather than using complex 3D modeling, the artificial intelligence tracks facial landmarks in the old picture—such as the eyes, eyebrows, nose, and lips—and coordinates their motion with the audio track.

The result is a fluid, expressive performance where the person in the photograph appears to be singing the lyrics, complete with realistic blinking and head tilts.

## How to Make an Old Photo Sing a Song Using AI

Transforming a historical photo into a singing video with Zoice takes only a few quick steps. Follow our guide below to get started.

### Step 1: Open the Zoice Dashboard

Launch your internet browser and navigate to [Zoice.com](https://zoice.com) to access the central creative platform.

![Zoice Homepage](/images/homepage.png)

### Step 2: Sign In to Your Creative Account

Select **Sign In** in the top-right corner. Enter your email and password, or proceed instantly using your linked Google account to access the creator dashboard.

![Sign In](/images/sign-in.png)

### Step 3: Create a New Zoice Account

If you are new to the platform, click **Sign Up** to configure a new creator profile. Fill in your name, email, and password to register.

![Sign Up](/images/sign-up.png)

### Step 4: Access the Avatar Panel

On the dashboard sidebar, select the **Avatar Characters** menu. If you have previously saved custom avatars, you can click on them, or click **Create New** to start with a fresh image.

![Avatar Character](/images/avatar-characters.png)

    * **Click on Create New**

![Create New](/images/ca-4.png)

### Step 5: Upload Your Vintage Photograph

To proceed, choose the **Upload a Photo** tab. Select a clear, high-resolution portrait from your files and name the character (for example, "1920s Jazz Portrait").

![Upload Photo](/images/ca-5.png)

For the best animation result, upload a front-facing image where the subject's face is clearly lit and visible. We uploaded this sample image:

![Used Image](/images/old-photo-image-upload.png)

Once saved, it will appear as a reusable character card in your library:

![Avatar Preview](/images/old-photo-preview.png) 

*Tip: Alternatively, you can toggle the **AI Prompt** tab to generate a custom vintage-style character using descriptive prompts (e.g., "Classic 1930s singer, black and white studio portrait").*

![AI Prompt](/images/ca-51.png)

### Step 6: Define Avatar Settings

Click **Avatar Setting** to specify basic properties like Name, Gender, and Age range. This metadata helps the model output more appropriate, natural facial expressions. Click save to store the settings.

![Avatar Settings](/images/old-photo-avatar-settings.png)

### Step 7: Click on Generate Video

Click the **Generate Video** button on your vintage avatar card to open the timeline interface and add your music track.

![Generate Video](/images/old-photo-generate-video-option.png)

### Step 8: Choose Generate from Audio

Toggle the dropdown menu from **Generate from script** to **Generate from audio**.

![Choose Generate from Audio](/images/old-photo-generate-from-audio-dropdown.png)

This will display two options: **Upload Audio** (to upload a song file from your device) and **Record** (to capture live audio directly).

### Step 9: Upload Your Song File

Click the **Upload Audio** button to upload the MP3 or WAV audio file that you want the character to sing. You can select a file from your computer or choose from Zoice's built-in media library.

![Generate from Audio](/images/old-photo-generate-from-audio.png)

### Step 10: Preview Your Track

Listen to the uploaded audio using the **Preview** button to ensure the track plays correctly and aligns with the desired start time.

![Preview Audio](/images/old-photo-preview-audio.png)

### Step 11: Set Output Dimensions

Adjust your video dimensions based on where you plan to share it:
* **Aspect Ratio**: Use 9:16 for vertical reels, TikTok, or Shorts; use 16:9 for landscape formats.
* **Resolution**: Select up to 4K resolution to maintain maximum detail of the original photo.

![Update Output Settings](/images/old-photo-update-output-settings.png)

### Step 12: Open the Action Prompts

Click the **Action Prompt** button at the bottom-right of the panel (next to the Output dimensions) to configure the facial movements and expressions of the person in the photo.

![Action Prompt](/images/old-photo-action-prompt.png)

### Step 13: Write a Custom Action Prompt

In the **Add action prompt** modal, write a custom description of the facial sways and expressions you want for the vintage photo on the left text field, or choose a template preset from the **Suggestions** list on the right (like *Professional* or *Casual/Warm*). Once satisfied, click **Add action** at the bottom-right of the modal.

![Action Prompt Selection](/images/old-photo-action-prompt-box.png)

### Step 14: Queue the Generation

Verify your selections and click **Generate** to queue the video rendering process.

![Singing Video Generation](/images/old-photo-generate.png)

### Step 15: Review the Audio Preview

Zoice provides an audio preview before compiling the final video. Check the pacing, and if it sounds good, click **Generate Avatar Video** to render the full animation.

![Generated Audio Preview](/images/old-photo-generated-audio-preview.png)

*Note: You can turn on **Enhance Audio** to filter background noise from older audio files before generating the final video.*

### Step 16: Download the Video

Once rendering is complete, click **Download** to save your new singing old photo video directly to your desktop.

![Download Video](/images/old-photo-download-video.png)

> [!NOTE]
> **Adding Background Music**: Zoice generates the lip-synced vocal performance video without background music tracks. To add background music or instrumental accompaniment to your singing old photo video, open any video editor (such as CapCut, Premiere Pro, or Canva), import your generated Zoice avatar video, and add your music track as an additional audio layer.

## Tips for Creating Better Old Photo Singing Videos

To get the highest quality animations when you make old photo sing a song, follow these styling tips:
* **Select Clear Faces**: Use front-facing vintage photos with clear facial features, good lighting, and open eyes for precise neural mapping.
* **Enhance Audio Quality**: Use the built-in Enhance Audio tool to clean historical recording noise and emphasize vocals.
* **Write Restored Expressions**: In the Action Prompt, specify natural head motions and soft blinking styles to keep the video looking realistic rather than artificial.
