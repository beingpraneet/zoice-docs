A singing AI old photo video allows you to animate historic black-and-white portraits, vintage pictures, or family keepsakes so they perform any song using artificial intelligence. You no longer need advanced animation skills or video editing tools to bring old photos to life.

With Zoice, you can upload an old photo, create an AI avatar character, add your favorite song or voice recording, and generate a natural-looking singing performance with accurate lip-syncing and expressive facial animations.

Whether you want to create a touching family video, a historical project, or engaging content for social media, AI singing old photos make it easy to generate unique animations in minutes.

In this article, you will learn how to make an old photo sing a song using Zoice by following a comprehensive, step-by-step tutorial.

## What is a Singing AI Old Photo?

A singing AI old photo is a digital animation of a static vintage photograph performing a song using artificial intelligence. Instead of using complex rigging tools, the AI analyzes the uploaded audio track and animates the facial features of the person in the photo, creating realistic lip movements, eye blinks, and natural head motions.

This technology helps users preserve memories, create funny clips, or produce educational content featuring historical figures singing or speaking in a polished format.

## How to Make an Old Photo Sing a Song Using AI

Animating an old photo to sing with Zoice takes only a few minutes. Follow the detailed steps below to get started.

### Step 1: Visit Zoice.com

Go to [Zoice.com](https://zoice.com) in your web browser. This is the main portal where you can access all of Zoice's AI creation tools.

![Zoice Homepage](/images/homepage.png)

### Step 2: Sign In to Your Zoice Account

Click the **Sign In** button in the top-right corner of the homepage. Enter your registered email address and password, or choose **Sign in with Google** to access your dashboard quickly.

![Sign In](/images/sign-in.png)

### Step 3: Create a New Zoice Account (For New Users)

If you don't have a Zoice account yet, click **Sign Up** on the login page. Fill in your details (name, email, and password) or select **Sign up with Google** to create a new account in one click.

![Sign Up](/images/sign-up.png)

### Step 4: Click Avatar Character

Select **Avatar Character** to begin creating a new AI old photo avatar. If you already have one saved, you can choose it from your library and proceed to the next step.

![Avatar Character](/images/avatar-characters.png)

    * **Click on Create New**

![Create New](/images/ca-4.png)

### Step 5: Upload or Generate Avatar Image

Now, you have two options to set up your old photo avatar:

* **Upload a Photo**: Upload a clear portrait image from your computer and give your singer a name.

![Upload Photo](/images/ca-5.png)

A high-quality, front-facing image helps Zoice generate more natural facial animations and accurate lip-syncing. We have uploaded an HD image and created an avatar, as shown below:

![Avatar Preview](/images/old-photo-preview.png) 

* **Use an AI Prompt**: Toggle the **AI Prompt** option.

![AI Prompt](/images/ca-51.png)

Enter a descriptive text prompt explaining what your avatar should look like (e.g., *"A vintage style black and white portrait of a 1920s jazz singer"*). Click **Generate Image**, review the generated image, name your avatar, and save it to your library.

### Step 6: Configure Avatar Settings (Optional)

After adding the image to your library, click **Avatar Setting** to add basic details such as Name, Gender, and Age. Providing these details can help organize your avatar library. Once done, save and close the avatar settings window.

![Avatar Settings](/images/old-photo-avatar-settings.png)

### Step 7: Click Generate Video

With your old photo avatar profile ready, click **Generate Video** to open the video editor and add your singing audio.

![Generate Video](/images/old-photo-generate-video-option.png)

### Step 8: Select Generate from Audio

Switch the input mode from **Generate from Script** to **Generate from Audio** to create a singing avatar using a sound file. You can upload a pre-recorded audio file such as a famous song, a funny voiceover, or an original music track.

![Generate from Audio](/images/old-photo-generate-from-audio.png)

### Step 9: Upload the Song

Upload the audio file (MP3, WAV, or M4A format) that you want your old photo avatar to sing. You can select a file from your device or choose a trending song from Zoice's built-in library.

![Upload Song](/images/old-photo-upload-song.png)

### Step 10: Preview Your Uploaded Audio

Once the audio is uploaded, click **Preview** to listen to the track and confirm that the audio has loaded correctly and plays from the right starting point.

![Preview Audio](/images/old-photo-preview-audio.png)

### Step 11: Update Output Settings

Configure the output settings to suit your target platform:

* **Aspect Ratio**: Choose the aspect ratio matching your target platform (e.g., 9:16 for portrait/TikTok/Shorts, 16:9 for landscape/YouTube).
* **Resolution**: Set the resolution up to 4K for maximum clarity.

![Update Output Settings](/images/old-photo-update-output-settings.png)

### Step 12: Configure the Action Prompt

Click **Action Prompt** to define the facial expressions and physical gestures of your singing old photo avatar during the video.

![Action Prompt](/images/old-photo-action-prompt.png)

### Step 13: Select or Write an Action Prompt

Write a custom prompt to specify your avatar's expressions (e.g., *"The singer performs enthusiastically, swaying head gently to the beat with nostalgic, emotional expressions"*).

![Action Prompt Selection](/images/old-photo-action-prompt-box.png)

### Step 14: Generate Your Singing Avatar Video

Once all settings are configured, click the **Generate** button to begin the generation process.

![Singing Video Generation](/images/old-photo-generate.png)

### Step 15: Preview the Generated Audio

Before processing the final video, Zoice generates an audio preview. Listen to the preview to verify the audio quality and timing. 

If the preview sounds correct, click **Generate Avatar Video** to render the final video.

![Generated Audio Preview](/images/old-photo-generated-audio.png)

**Tip**: You can toggle the **Enhance Audio** switch in the top-right corner of the script box to automatically improve the sound quality of the uploaded track before rendering.

### Step 16: Download Your Singing AI Avatar Video

Once the rendering is complete and you are satisfied with the result, click the **Download** button to save the singing old photo video to your device. It is now ready to share on social media or send to friends.

![Download Video](/images/old-photo-download-video.png)
