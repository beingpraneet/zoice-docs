In today’s competitive e-commerce and digital marketing landscape, visual product presentation is key to capturing customer attention and driving sales. Static product photos and text descriptions are no longer enough. Modern buyers respond best to video demonstrations where a spokesperson introduces the product, highlights key features, and explains benefits.

With Zoice, you don't need expensive video production setups, cameras, or hired actors to feature products in your video content. By combining AI avatars with product placement prompts and tailored scripts, you can seamlessly add physical products, digital goods, or brand merchandise to your AI presenter.

In this tutorial, you will learn how to add products to your avatar using Zoice through a complete step-by-step guide.

## What is a Product-Integrated AI Avatar?

A product-integrated AI avatar is a digital presenter customized to showcase, hold, interact with, or promote specific products in an AI-generated video. Whether you are running an e-commerce store, launching a SaaS tool, or advertising physical merchandise, your avatar acts as an automated product demonstrator.

Key capabilities of product-integrated AI avatars include:

- **Product Placement Prompts**: Directing your AI avatar to interact with or feature specific items (e.g., *holding a modern smartwatch*, *pointing to a skincare bottle*, or *wearing branded apparel*).
- **Synchronized Product Demos**: Matching speech delivery and hand gestures to key product callouts in your script.
- **Customized Branding**: Tailoring avatar outfits, studio backgrounds, and product surroundings to align with your brand identity.
- **Multilingual Product Pitches**: Generating the same product demonstration video in over 100 languages with localized AI voices.

## Key Benefits of Adding Products to Your Avatar

Integrating products into your AI avatar videos offers significant advantages for creators, marketers, and business owners:

- **Higher Conversion Rates**: Product videos led by a friendly presenter increase trust, engagement, and click-through rates.
- **Cost & Time Savings**: Eliminate expensive studio rentals, filming schedules, and voice actor fees.
- **Consistent Brand Identity**: Use the same digital ambassador across all your product launches and promotional campaigns.
- **Scalable Content Production**: Generate dozens of product demonstration videos for your entire catalog in hours instead of weeks.

## How to Add Products to Your Avatar Using Zoice

Follow the step-by-step process below to create a video featuring products with your AI avatar.

### Step 1: Visit Zoice.com and Click Sign In

Go to [Zoice.com](https://zoice.com) in your web browser and click the **Sign In** button in the top-right corner of the homepage.

![Zoice Homepage](/images/homepage.png)

### Step 2: Sign In to Your Zoice Account

Enter your registered email address and password, then click **Sign In**. Alternatively, use **Sign in with Google** for single-click authentication.

![Sign In](/images/sign-in.png)

### Step 3: Create a New Zoice Account (For New Users)

If you are new to Zoice, click **Sign Up** to register a new account.

![Sign Up](/images/sign-up.png)

Fill in your details or sign up using your Google account to access the main dashboard.

### Step 4: Navigate to Avatar Characters

On the left navigation panel of your Zoice dashboard, click **Avatar Characters** under the **Manage** section.

![Avatar Characters Dashboard](/images/avatar-characters.png)

### Step 5: Click Create New Avatar

Click the **Create New** button to launch the avatar creator tool.

![Create New Avatar](/images/ca-4.png)

### Step 6: Upload Image or Generate Avatar with Product Placement

You can choose between two methods to add a product to your avatar setup:

- **Upload Image**: Upload a high-resolution portrait photo of your presenter holding or posing next to your product.
- **AI Prompt**: Switch to the **AI Prompt** tab and enter a detailed prompt incorporating your product (e.g., *"A professional female presenter holding a sleek black smartphone against a modern office backdrop, realistic lighting"*).

![Upload Image](/images/add-product-upload-image.png)

![AI Prompt Option](/images/ca-51.png)

### Step 7: Configure Avatar Profile Settings

Click **Avatar Setting** to define your avatar’s profile details, such as Name, Gender, Age, and Category. Setting accurate profile attributes ensures smooth animation and facial rendering around your product.

![Avatar Settings](/images/add-product-avatar-settings.png)

### Step 8: Click Generate Video

Once your product avatar profile is saved, select your character from the library and click **Generate Video**.

![Generate Video](/images/add-product-generate-video-option.png)

### Step 9: Add Script or Audio Content

Click on the script area in the video creation studio.

![Click on Script Box](/images/add-product-click-on-script-box.png)

The script box will expand so you can write or paste the script for your avatar. Structure your script to introduce the product, highlight key features, and end with a clear Call to Action (CTA).

![Add Script](/images/add-product-add-script.png)

### Step 10: Select an AI Voice for Your Product Presenter

Click on **Select Voice** to choose an AI voice for your presenter.

![Click on Select Voice](/images/add-product-click-on-select-voice.png)

A pop-up window will open where you can add your own voice or choose from public AI voices as per your needs. Select your preferred voice model and click **Add Voice**.

![Choose Voice](/images/add-product-choose-voice.png)

### Step 11: Configure Output Settings

Adjust your video resolution, aspect ratio (16:9 for YouTube/Web, 9:16 for TikTok/Reels), and frame rate to match your distribution platform.

![Output Settings](/images/add-product-update-output-settings.png)

### Step 12: Add Product Action Prompts

Click on **Action Prompt** to control how your AI avatar behaves during the product presentation.

![Click Action Prompt](/images/add-product-click-action-prompt.png)

This will open the action prompt box where you can type your custom action instructions or select from existing action prompt templates (such as holding the product, gesturing features, or smiling warmly).

![Action Prompt Box](/images/add-product-action-prompt-box.png)

### Step 13: Click Generate

Click **Generate** to start processing your product avatar video setup.

![Generate Video](/images/add-product-video-generation.png)

### Step 14: Preview Audio and Click Generate Avatar Video

Once you click **Generate**, the AI voice track is generated first. Listen to the audio preview to verify that the voice tone, speech pacing, and pronunciation are generated properly. If everything sounds right, click **Generate Avatar Video** to render the video, and wait a few minutes for the final video generation to complete.

![Preview Audio](/images/add-product-generated-audio.png)

### Step 15: Preview and Download Your Video

Once processing is complete, play your video preview to verify speech timing and visual presentation. Click **Download** to save the final video file.

![Download Video](/images/add-product-download-video.png)

## Best Practices for Avatar Product Presentations

To maximize conversions and engagement with your AI product avatar videos:

- **Keep Scripts Concise**: Hook viewers in the first 3 seconds by showing the product immediately.
- **Use Clear Lighting**: Ensure your source image has strong lighting on both the avatar and the featured product.
- **Match Voice Persona**: Pick an energetic voice for fitness/tech products and a reassuring voice for wellness/finance items.
- **Include Strong CTAs**: Clearly direct viewers to click your link, visit your store, or purchase the product.
