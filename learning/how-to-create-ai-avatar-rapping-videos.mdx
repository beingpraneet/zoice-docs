An AI avatar rapping video allows you to transform a simple portrait into a realistic avatar that performs fast-flowing rap verses using artificial intelligence. You no longer need professional recording equipment or advanced video editing skills to create engaging hip-hop videos.

With Zoice, you can upload a photo, create an AI avatar, add your favorite rap track or voice recording, and generate a natural-looking rapping performance with accurate lip-syncing and expressive facial animations.

Whether you are a content creator, musician, marketer, educator, or business owner, AI rapping avatars make it easy to generate unique videos for social media, marketing campaigns, and digital entertainment.

In this article, you will learn how to create an AI avatar rapping video using Zoice by following a comprehensive, step-by-step tutorial.

## What is a Rapping AI Avatar?

A rapping AI avatar is a digital character that performs rap songs using artificial intelligence. Instead of recording yourself on camera, the AI analyzes your uploaded audio and animates an avatar with realistic lip movements, fast vocal synchronization, and natural head motions.

Rapping AI avatars help creators produce engaging videos in minutes while maintaining a polished, professional appearance. They are widely used for music content, promotional videos, social shorts, and digital entertainment.

## How to Create AI Avatar Rapping Videos Using AI

Creating an AI avatar rapping video with Zoice takes only a few minutes. To begin, create or choose an AI avatar, upload your rap audio, configure the output settings, and generate your video. Follow the detailed steps below to get started.

### Step 1: Visit Zoice.com

Go to [Zoice.com](https://zoice.com) in your web browser. This is the main portal where you can access all of Zoice's AI creation tools.

![Zoice Homepage](/images/homepage.png)

### Step 2: Sign In to Your Zoice Account

Click the **Sign In** button in the top-right corner of the homepage. Enter your registered email address and password, or choose **Sign in with Google** to access your dashboard quickly.

![Sign In](/images/sign-in.png)

### Step 3: Create a New Zoice Account (For New Users)

If you don't have a Zoice account yet, click **Sign Up** on the login page. Fill in your details (name, email, and password) or select **Sign up with Google** to create a new account in one click.

![Sign Up](/images/sign-up.png)

### Step 4: Click Avatar Characters

Click **Avatar Characters** to begin creating a new AI avatar. If you already have an avatar character, you can choose it from your library and proceed to the next step.

Creating a custom avatar allows you to generate personalized rapping videos that match your appearance or brand identity.

![Avatar Character](/images/avatar-characters.png)

* **Click on Create New**

![Create New](/images/ca-4.png)

### Step 5: Upload or Generate Avatar Image

Now, you have two options to set up your AI rapper avatar:

* **Upload a Photo**: Upload a clear portrait image from your computer and give your rapper a name.

![Upload Photo](/images/ca-5.png)

A high-quality, front-facing image helps Zoice generate more natural facial animations and accurate lip-syncing. We used the below image:

![Upload Photo](/images/rapper-avatar-generate.png)

* **Use an AI Prompt**: Toggle the **AI Prompt** option.

![AI Prompt](/images/ca-51.png)

Enter a descriptive text prompt explaining what your avatar should look like (e.g., *"A cool hip-hop rapper in a studio, cinematic lighting"*). Click **Generate Image**, review the generated image, name your avatar, and save it to your library.

### Step 6: Configure Avatar Settings (Optional)

After adding the image to your library, click **Avatar Setting** to add basic details such as Name, Gender, Age, and Ethnicity. Providing these details helps achieve better, more personalized results. Once done, save and close the avatar settings window.

![Avatar Settings](/images/rapper-avatar-settings.png)

### Step 7: Click Generate Video

With your avatar profile ready, click **Generate Video** to open the video editor and add your rapping audio.

![Avatar Preview](/images/rapper-generate-video.png)

### Step 8: Select Generate from Audio

Switch the input mode from **Generate from Script** to **Generate from Audio** to create a rapping avatar using a sound file. You can either record your voice directly within Zoice or upload a pre-recorded audio file (such as a rap verse or vocal track).

![Generate from Audio](/images/rapper-generate-from-audio.png)

### Step 9: Upload or Record Audio

Select **Upload Audio** to upload a pre-recorded rap verse or song stem from your computer, or click **Record Audio** to record your rap voiceover directly.

![Upload or Record Audio](/images/rapper-upload-audio.png)

Once the audio file is uploaded or recorded, click **Preview** to listen to the rap track and verify that the sound and timing loaded correctly.

![Preview Audio](/images/rapper-preview-audio-uploaded.png)

### Step 10: Update Output Settings

Configure the output settings to suit your target platform:

* **Aspect Ratio**: Choose the aspect ratio matching your target platform (e.g., 9:16 for portrait/social shorts, 16:9 for landscape/YouTube).
* **Resolution**: Set the resolution up to 4K for maximum clarity.

![Update Output Settings](/images/rapper-update-output-settings.png)

### Step 11: Configure the Action Prompt

Click **Action Prompt** to define the facial expressions and physical gestures of your rapping avatar.

![Click Action Prompt](/images/rapper-action-prompt.png)

This will open the action prompt box where you can select one of the suggested action prompts or write a custom prompt to specify your avatar's expressions (such as *"confident rapper nodding to the beat with fast lip-syncing and energetic stage presence"*).

![Action Prompt Box](/images/rapper-action-prompt-box.png)

### Step 12: Generate Your Rapping Avatar Video

Once all settings are configured, click the **Generate** button to begin the generation process.

![Rapping Video Generation](/images/rapper-video-generate.png)

### Step 13: Preview the Generated Audio

Before processing the final video, Zoice generates an audio preview. Listen to the preview to verify:

* **Audio Quality**: Ensure there is no noise or distortion.
* **Voice Pacing**: Verify the fast rhythm and timing.
* **Overall Flow**: Confirm it aligns with your expectations.

If the preview sounds correct, click **Generate Avatar Video** to render the final video.

![Generated Audio Preview](/images/rapper-generated-audio.png)

**Tip**: You can toggle the **Enhance Audio** switch in the top-right corner of the script box to automatically improve the sound quality of the generated voice before rendering.

### Step 14: Download Your Rapping AI Avatar Video

Once the rendering is complete and you are satisfied with the result, click the **Download** button to save the video to your device. It is now ready to share or publish.

![Download Video](/images/download-ai-avatar-rapper-video.png)

> [!NOTE]
> **Adding Background Music**: Zoice generates the lip-synced vocal performance video without background music tracks. To add background music or beat tracks to your avatar video, open any video editor (such as CapCut, Premiere Pro, or Canva), import your generated Zoice avatar video, and add your music track as an additional audio layer.
