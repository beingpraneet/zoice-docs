In this tutorial, we will use an old portrait of my great-grandfather to demonstrate how to animate a vintage photo and convert it into a realistic talking video step by step.

## What is AI Family Photo Animation?

AI family photo animation is the process of using deep learning neural networks to transform static family portraits into realistic talking avatar videos. The AI model detects facial landmarks (such as eyes, nose, mouth, and jawline) in vintage photographs and applies natural micro-expressions, head tilts, and lip movement.

Key capabilities of AI family photo animation include:

- **Vintage Portrait Restoration & Morphs**: Animating both colored and black-and-white family portraits.
- **Lip-Sync Accuracy**: Matching spoken family stories or historical letters to mouth movements.
- **Natural Facial Expressions**: Adding gentle head sways, eye blinks, and warm smiles with Action Prompts.
- **Voice Matching**: Pairing vintage photos with custom voice clones or realistic AI voice presets across various age groups and regional accents.

## Key Benefits of Animating Old Family Photos

Animating old family pictures offers unique emotional and creative benefits:

- **Emotional Tributes**: Create touching tribute videos for birthdays, anniversaries, or memorial celebrations.
- **Easy & Fast Creation**: Generate high-definition talking avatar videos in just a few clicks without manual video editing.

## How to Animate Old Family Photos Using AI with Zoice

Follow the detailed step-by-step instructions below to animate your old family photos using your great-grandfather's portrait as an example.

### Step 1: Open the Zoice Homepage

Launch your web browser, navigate to [Zoice.com](https://zoice.com), and click **Sign In** in the top-right corner.

![Zoice Homepage](/images/homepage.png)

### Step 2: Sign In to Your Dashboard Account

Enter your email and password, then click **Sign In**. Or select the **Sign in with Google** button for quick dashboard access.

![Sign In](/images/sign-in.png)

### Step 3: Set Up a Free Creator Profile

If you are a first-time user, select **Sign Up** on the page to register your free workspace.

![Sign Up](/images/sign-up.png)

### Step 4: Click Avatar Characters

Locate the left sidebar on the main dashboard page and select the **Avatar Characters** menu.

![Avatar Characters Dashboard](/images/avatar-characters.png)

### Step 5: Click Create New

On the presenter library page, click the **Create New** button to set up your great-grandfather's avatar card.

![Create New Avatar](/images/ca-4.png)

### Step 6: Upload Your Great-Grandfather's Portrait Photo and Generate Avatar

Select the **Upload Image** option on the creation page. Enter a name for your great-grandfather in the **Avatar Name** field (e.g., 'Old Photo Talking'), upload or drag-and-drop the face portrait in the **Upload Base Image** box, and click **Generate Avatar** at the bottom to build his AI profile.

![Upload Image](/images/ca-5.png)

* We used this image for the great-grandfather's portrait:

![Great-Grandfather Portrait](/images/relative-portrait-upload-preview.png)

### Step 7: Select Your Great-Grandfather's Avatar from Avatar Characters List

Go back to the Avatar Characters menu. Your new card will appear in your library. Click on the card to select it.

![Select Avatar](/images/create-talking-photo-of-deceased-select-avatar.png)

### Step 8: Configure Avatar Settings

Click **Avatar Setting** next to Generate Video. Under the **General** settings, add the Name, Gender, Age, and Ethnicity of your great-grandfather to help the AI map natural expressions. Click Save when finished.

![Avatar Settings](/images/create-talking-photo-of-deceased-avatar-settings.png)

### Step 9: Click Generate Video

With your character card active, click the **Generate Video** button to launch the avatar advance configuration.

![Generate Video](/images/create-talking-photo-of-deceased-generate-video-option.png)

### Step 10: Click on the Script Field

In the editor timeline, click on the **Script** tab to open the text editing panel.

![Click on Script box to add Script](/images/create-talking-photo-of-deceased-click-on-script-box.png)

### Step 11: Write Your Script

Type the exact message, family legacy story, or wisdom you want your great-grandfather to speak.

![Add Script](/images/create-talking-photo-of-deceased-add-script.png)

*Tip: For old family photos, write warm reflections on family origins, life advice, or historical memories.*

### Step 12: Select a Voice Model

Click on **Select Voice**. Select a voice preset that matches the gender, age, and accent of your great-grandfather, or upload a custom voice sample.

![Select Voice](/images/create-talking-photo-of-deceased-click-on-select-voice.png)

* We used the below voice for the great-grandfather in the example:

![Choose Voice](/images/create-talking-photo-of-deceased-choose-voice.png)

You can select a public AI voice model, record live audio, or upload a pre-recorded voice file.

### Step 13: Choose Output Formats

Under **Output Settings**, choose the aspect ratio:
- **Aspect Ratio**: Select 9:16 for mobile phone sharing (WhatsApp, Reels, Shorts) or 16:9 for widescreen archives.
- **Resolution**: Choose 1080p or 4K to preserve the fine details of the original photo.

![Update Output Settings](/images/create-talking-photo-of-deceased-output-settings.png)

### Step 14: Select Action Prompt

Click on **Action Prompt** to control the physical behaviors. Write custom instructions (e.g., *"Gentle speaking style, nodding head slightly, warm gaze, smiling softly"*).

![Click on Action Prompt](/images/create-talking-photo-of-deceased-click-on-action-prompt.png)

* We used the below action prompt for the great-grandfather in the example:

![Action Prompt](/images/create-talking-photo-of-deceased-action-prompt.png)

### Step 15: Click on Generate for Video Generation

Verify your text script and configurations, and click **Generate** to start processing.

![Generate Video](/images/create-talking-photo-of-deceased-video-generation.png)

### Step 16: Confirm the Audio Preview

Listen to the audio preview to check speech tone and pacing. If everything sounds correct, click **Generate Avatar Video** to compile the video clip.

![Generated Audio Preview](/images/create-talking-photo-of-deceased-audio-preview.png)

### Step 17: Download the Video

Once processing is complete, check the animations. Click the **Download** button to save the MP4 video to your device.

![Download Video](/images/download-create-talking-photo-of-deceased-video.png)

## Best Practices for Animating Old Family Photos

To ensure the best outcomes when you animate old family photos, follow these best practices:

- **Restore Photo Quality First**: If the original picture is faded or scratched, use an image restoration tool before uploading to ensure the AI detects facial landmarks accurately.
- **Select Appropriate Voices**: Choose older, warm, or classic voice styles from the library to preserve the historic feel.
- **Add Contextual Backdrops**: Render the avatar with a green screen, then place a vintage room or sepia background behind the speaker in post-production.
- **Craft Authentic Scripts**: Write scripts that capture your family member's genuine personality, favorite sayings, or historical life lessons.
