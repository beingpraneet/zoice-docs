In the competitive world of real estate, high-quality video content is essential for attracting buyers and selling properties faster. However, constantly filming property tours, market updates, and neighborhood guides can be expensive and time-consuming for busy agents.

With artificial intelligence, real estate professionals can now generate realistic video content without stepping in front of a camera. Using AI avatar technology, you can create a digital real estate agent to present your properties, explain market trends, and introduce your agency.

Instead of spending hours filming multiple takes, you can simply type a script and let a professional AI avatar deliver the presentation with flawless speech and engaging body language.

In this article, you will learn how to create a real estate AI avatar video using Zoice by following a complete step-by-step process.

## What Are Real Estate AI Avatar Videos?

Real estate AI avatar videos are digital presentations created using artificial intelligence. They feature a lifelike virtual real estate agent that speaks directly to potential buyers, renters, or sellers.

You write the property description or market update, and the AI animates the avatar to deliver the information naturally. These avatars can be placed over images of houses, neighborhood drone shots, or office backgrounds.

For example, you can create an AI avatar video for:

*“Welcome to this beautiful 3-bedroom home in the heart of downtown…”*
or
*“Here is your local real estate market update for November…”*

The AI avatar reads the script flawlessly, creating a highly professional and engaging video that helps you close more deals.

## How to Create a Real Estate AI Avatar Video Using Zoice

Creating a real estate video with an AI avatar is simple. Follow the steps below to generate a professional property presentation.

### Step 1: Visit Zoice.com and Click Sign In

Go to [Zoice.com](https://zoice.com) in your browser and click the **Sign In** button available at the top-right corner of the homepage. This will open the login page where you can access your Zoice account.

![Zoice Homepage](/images/homepage.png)

### Step 2: Sign In to Your Zoice Account

If you already have a Zoice account, enter your registered email address and password, then click **Sign In**.

You can also choose **Sign in with Google** if your account is connected with Google. After successfully signing in, you will be redirected to the Zoice dashboard.

![Sign In](/images/sign-in.png)

### Step 3: Create a New Zoice Account (For New Users)

If you are using Zoice for the first time, click **Sign Up** to create a new account.

![Sign Up](/images/sign-up.png)

Enter the required details:
- Name
- Email address
- Password

You can also select **Sign up with Google** to quickly create your account. Once your account is created, you can access the Zoice dashboard and start creating AI real estate videos.

### Step 4: Navigate to Avatar Characters

After logging in, you will reach the Zoice dashboard. From the left sidebar, click **Avatar Characters** under the Manage section.

![Avatar Characters Dashboard](/images/avatar-characters.png)

This section allows you to create, customize, and manage AI avatars that you can use for property tours, agency introductions, and other real estate marketing content.

### Step 5: Click Create New Avatar

On the Avatar Characters page, click the **Create New** button to start creating your custom AI real estate agent.

![Create New Avatar](/images/ca-4.png)

This will open the avatar creation page where you can create your avatar by uploading an image or generating one using an AI prompt.

### Step 6A: Create Your Avatar Using an Image (Skip to step 6B if you don't have an image)

Upload a clear, high-quality image of yourself, a colleague, or a professional model that you want to convert into a real estate avatar. Add an avatar name so you can easily identify it later in your library.

![Upload Image](/images/ca-5.png)

For better results, use an image with:
- Clear face visibility
- Professional business attire
- A welcoming, friendly smile
- High image quality

### Step 6B: Generate Avatar Using AI Prompt (Skip if you uploaded an image)

If you don't have an image, select the **AI Prompt** option to create an avatar using a text description. Enter an Avatar Name, describe the type of real estate agent you want (for example: *"A friendly and professional female real estate agent in a modern blazer, standing in a luxury home living room, bright natural lighting"*), and click **Generate Image**. Once you are happy with the generated image, click **Generate Avatar**.

![AI Prompt](/images/ca-51.png)

### Step 7: Upload Your Avatar Image and Generate Avatar

Now upload the image you want to convert into your AI real estate agent. Add a unique **Avatar Name** so you can easily find and manage your avatar later, and click **Generate Avatar**. Zoice will process your image and create your AI avatar profile.

![Generate Avatar](/images/real-estate-upload-image.png)

*Note: The images referenced are examples; your interface will reflect your uploaded agent image.*

### Step 8: Select Your Created Avatar

After your AI avatar profile is generated, go back to the Avatar Characters section. Your newly created avatar will appear in the avatar library. Click on the avatar you created to select it.

![Select Avatar](/images/real-estate-select-avatar.png)

### Step 9: Go to Avatar settings (Optional)

Click on Avatar Setting to add details about the AI Agent, like Gender, Ethnicity, Age, Voice, and Accent.

![Avatar Settings](/images/real-estate-avatar-settings.png)

### Step 10: Click Generate Video

After selecting the avatar you want to use for your property video from the library, click **Generate Video** to open the video creation workspace page.

![Generate Video](/images/real-estate-generate-video-option.png)

### Step 11: Add Script and Configure Video Settings

After opening the avatar video creator, you can customize your video by entering a script, selecting a voice, choosing the output format, and adding an action prompt.

![Click on Script box to add Script](/images/real-estate-click-on-script-box.png)

### Step 12: Add Your Script and Choose Voice Model

Click on the script box. A popup window will appear where you can write the detailed property script. Add the script that your AI agent will speak in the video. Choose the Voice Model from the right dropdown, and click on Add Script.

![Add Script](/images/real-estate-add-script.png)

*Tip: For real estate videos, use persuasive, inviting, and descriptive language to highlight the best features of a property.*

### Step 13: Click on Select Voice

Click on the **Select Voice** option. You can browse through the available public voices, listen to voice previews, or upload your own voice. Select a friendly, trustworthy, and articulate AI voice model that matches a professional real estate agent's tone.

![Select Voice](/images/real-estate-click-on-select-voice.png)

  * **Choose the Voice for your AI real estate agent**

    ![Choose Voice](/images/real-estate-choose-voice.png)

### Step 14: Choose Video Output Settings

Click on **Output Settings**. Choose the aspect ratio and resolution based on where you want to publish your real estate video:
- **Aspect Ratio:** 16:9 for YouTube property tours, Zillow listings, or website embeds, or 9:16 for TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts (ideal for quick property teasers).
- **Resolution:** Select 1080p or up to 4K for high-quality professional output.

![Update Output Settings](/images/real-estate-output-settings.png)

### Step 15: Add Action Prompt

Click on **Action Prompt** to control how your AI avatar behaves during the presentation. You can write your own instructions or select suggested action styles (such as: *"warm, welcoming smile, open and inviting hand gestures, looking directly at the viewer"*).

![Click on Action Prompt](/images/real-estate-click-on-action-prompt.png)

* **Now add an action prompt for the avatar.**

![Action Prompt](/images/real-estate-action-prompt.png)

### Step 16: Generate Your AI Real Estate Video

After setting up the script, voice, output settings, and action prompt, review all your configuration options. Click **Generate** to start processing your AI property video.

![Generate Video](/images/real-estate-video-generation.png)

### Step 17: Preview Audio and Click on Generate Avatar Video

Before generating the final video, Zoice creates an audio preview so you can verify how your script sounds. Check pronunciation of street names and property features. If everything sounds correct, click **Generate Avatar Video** to create the final talking video.

![Generated Audio Preview](/images/real-estate-audio-preview.png)

### Step 18: Preview and Download Your AI Real Estate Video

Once the AI avatar video finishes processing, play the final result to check the avatar movements, voice synchronization, and video quality.

![Download Video](/images/download-ai-real-estate-video.png)

If you are satisfied, click **Download** to save the video to your device. Your AI real estate video is now ready to be uploaded to your property listings, social media, or email newsletters!

## Tips for Creating Better AI Real Estate Videos

* **Use Property Photos as Backgrounds:** Generate your avatar with a transparent or green screen background, then use a video editor to place them over photos of the actual property you are selling.
* **Keep Descriptions Vivid:** Use descriptive words like "sun-drenched," "spacious," and "newly renovated" to help buyers visualize the home.
* **Include a Call to Action:** Always end your script by telling the viewer what to do next (e.g., "Call us today," "Click the link in the bio," or "Swipe up to view the full listing").
* **Maintain High Quality:** Property buyers value aesthetics. Always render your videos in 1080p or 4k resolution for crisp, professional visuals.
