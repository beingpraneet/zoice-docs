A singing AI avatar video allows you to transform a simple portrait into a realistic avatar that performs any song using artificial intelligence. You no longer need professional recording equipment or advanced video editing skills to create engaging singing videos.

With Zoice, you can upload a photo, create an AI avatar, add your favorite song or voice recording, and generate a natural-looking singing performance with accurate lip-syncing and expressive facial animations.

Whether you are a content creator, musician, marketer, educator, or business owner, AI singing avatars make it easy to generate unique videos for social media, marketing campaigns, and digital entertainment.

In this article, you will learn how to create a singing AI avatar video using Zoice by following a comprehensive, step-by-step tutorial.

## What is a Singing AI Avatar?

A singing AI avatar is a digital character that performs songs using artificial intelligence. Instead of recording yourself on camera, the AI analyzes your uploaded audio and animates an avatar with realistic lip movements, facial expressions, and natural head motions.

Singing AI avatars help creators produce engaging videos in minutes while maintaining a polished, professional appearance. They are widely used for music content, promotional videos, educational projects, and social media posts.

## How to Make a Singing AI Avatar Video Using AI

Creating a singing AI avatar video with Zoice takes only a few minutes. To begin, create or choose an AI avatar, upload your audio, configure the output settings, and generate your video. Follow the detailed steps below to get started.

### Step 1: Visit Zoice.com

Go to [Zoice.com](https://zoice.com) in your web browser. This is the main portal where you can access all of Zoice's AI creation tools.

![Zoice Homepage](/images/homepage.png)

### Step 2: Sign In to Your Zoice Account

Click the **Sign In** button in the top-right corner of the homepage. Enter your registered email address and password, or choose **Sign in with Google** to access your dashboard quickly.

![Sign In](/images/sign-in.png)

### Step 3: Create a New Zoice Account (For New Users)

If you don't have a Zoice account yet, click **Sign Up** on the login page. Fill in your details (name, email, and password) or select **Sign up with Google** to create a new account in one click.

![Sign Up](/images/sign-up.png)

### Step 4: Click Avatar Character

Click **Avatar Characters** to begin creating a new AI avatar. If you already have an avatar character, you can choose it from your library and proceed to the next step.

Creating a custom avatar allows you to generate personalized singing videos that match your appearance or brand identity.

![Avatar Character](/images/avatar-characters.png)

    * **Click on Create New**

![Create New](/images/ca-4.png)

### Step 5: Upload or Generate Avatar Image

Now, You have two options to set up your AI singing avatar:

* **Upload a Photo**: Upload a clear portrait image from your computer and give your singer a name.

![Upload Photo](/images/ca-5.png)

A high-quality, front-facing image helps Zoice generate more natural facial animations and accurate lip-syncing. We used the below image:

![Upload Photo](/images/singing-avatar-preview.png)

* **Use an AI Prompt**: Toggle the **AI Prompt** option.

![AI Prompt](/images/ca-51.png)

Enter a descriptive text prompt explaining what your avatar should look like (e.g., *"A professional male singer in a recording studio, warm lighting"*). Click **Generate Image**, review the generated image, name your avatar, and save it to your library.

### Step 6: Configure Avatar Settings (Optional)

After adding the image to your library, click **Avatar Setting** to add basic details such as Name, Gender, Age, and Ethnicity. Providing these details helps achieve better, more personalized results. Once done, save and close the avatar settings window.

![Avatar Settings](/images/singing-avatar-settings.png)

### Step 7: Click Generate Video

With your avatar profile ready, click **Generate Video** to open the video editor and add your singing audio.

![Avatar Preview](/images/singing-generate-video.png)

### Step 8: Select Generate from Audio

Switch the input mode from **Generate from Script** to **Generate from Audio** to create a singing avatar using a sound file. You can either record your voice directly within Zoice or upload a pre-recorded audio file (such as a song or vocal track).

![Generate from Audio](/images/singing-generate-from-audio.png)

### Step 9: Preview Your Uploaded Audio

Once the audio is uploaded, click **Preview** to listen to the track and confirm that the audio has loaded correctly.

![Preview Audio](/images/singing-preview-audio.png)

### Step 10: Update Output Settings

Configure the output settings to suit your target platform:

* **Aspect Ratio**: Choose the aspect ratio matching your target platform (e.g., 9:16 for portrait/social shorts, 16:9 for landscape/YouTube).
* **Resolution**: Set the resolution up to 4K for maximum clarity.

![Update Output Settings](/images/singing-update-output-settings.png)

### Step 11: Configure the Action Prompt

Click **Action Prompt** to define the facial expressions and physical gestures of your singing avatar during the video.

![Action Prompt](/images/singing-action-prompt.png)

### Step 12: Select or Write an Action Prompt

Select one of the suggested action prompts or write a custom prompt to specify your avatar's expressions (e.g., *"smiling and winking while singing with passion"*).

![Action Prompt Selection](/images/singing-action-prompt-box.png)

### Step 13: Generate Your Singing Avatar Video

Once all settings are configured, click the **Generate** button to begin the generation process.

![Singing Video Generation](/images/singing-video-generate.png)

### Step 14: Preview the Generated Audio

Before processing the final video, Zoice generates an audio preview. Listen to the preview to verify:

* **Audio Quality**: Ensure there is no noise or distortion.
* **Voice Pacing**: Verify the rhythm and timing.
* **Overall Flow**: Confirm it aligns with your expectations.

If the preview sounds correct, click **Generate Avatar Video** to render the final video.

**Tip**: You can toggle the **Enhance Audio** switch in the top-right corner of the script box to automatically improve the sound quality of the generated voice before rendering.

### Step 15: Download Your Singing AI Avatar Video

Once the rendering is complete and you are satisfied with the result, click the **Download** button to save the video to your device. It is now ready to share or publish.

![Download Video](/images/download-ai-avatar-singing-video.png)
