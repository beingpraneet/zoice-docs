A singing AI avatar video lets you transform a simple portrait into a realistic avatar that performs any song using artificial intelligence. You no longer need professional recording equipment or video editing skills to create engaging singing videos.

With Zoice, you can upload a photo, create an AI avatar, add your favorite song or voice recording, and generate a natural looking singing performance with accurate lip synchronization and expressive facial animations.

Whether you are a content creator, musician, marketer, educator, or business owner, AI singing avatars make it easy to create unique videos for social media, marketing campaigns, and entertainment.

In this article, you will learn how to make a singing AI avatar video using AI with Zoice by following a complete step by step process.

## What is a Singing AI Avatar?

A singing AI avatar is a digital character that performs songs using artificial intelligence. Instead of recording yourself on camera, AI analyzes your uploaded audio and animates an avatar with realistic lip movements, facial expressions, and subtle head motions.

Singing AI avatars help creators produce engaging videos in minutes while maintaining a professional appearance. They are widely used for music content, promotional videos, educational projects, and social media posts.

## How to Make a Singing AI Avatar Video Using AI

Creating a singing AI avatar video with Zoice only takes a few minutes. First, create or choose an AI avatar, upload your song, customize the output settings, and generate your video. Follow the detailed steps below to create your own singing AI avatar.

### Step 1: Sign in to Your Zoice Account

Visit the Zoice website and sign in to your account. If you are a new user, create an account using your email address or another available sign in option. After signing in, you will be redirected to the Zoice dashboard where all AI creation tools are available.

![Sign In](../../images/homepage.png)

### Step 2: Go to See All Tools

From the left sidebar, click on **See All Tools**.

![See All Tools](../../images/ca-1.png)

### Step 3: Scroll down and select **AI Avatar Videos**.

Once you see the option click on it, then the custum avatr section well apper.

![AI Avatar Dashboard](../../images/ca-2.png)

### Step 4: Click Avatar Character

Select **Avatar Character** to begin creating a new AI avatar. If you already have an avatar character, you can simply choose it and continue to the next step.

Creating a custom avatar allows you to generate personalized singing videos that match your own appearance or brand.

![Avatar Character](../../images/ca-3.png)

### Step 5: Upload or Generate Avatar Image

You have two options to set up your AI Singing avatar image:

* Upload a clear portrait image from your computer and name the singer.

![Upload Photo](../../images/ca-5.png)

A high quality image helps Zoice create more natural facial animations and accurate lip synchronization.

* Toggle the **AI Prompt**

![AI Prompt](../../images/ca-51.png)

Enter a text prompt describing the desired avatar. Click **Generate Image** to create the avatar. Review the generated image, give a name and save it to your library.

### Step 6: Configure Avatar Settings (Optional)

Once added your image to library, click **Avatar Setting** to add the basic details like Name, Gender, Age, and Ethnicity of the avatar for better result. Save and Close avatar setting window.

![Avatar Settings](../../images/ca-6.png)

### Step 7: Click Generate Video

Now your avatar profile is ready to make a singing video, now you can add singing audio to your avatar.

![Avatar Preview](../../images/ca-8.png)

### Step 8: Select Generate from Audio

Change the **Generate from Script** to **Generate from Audio** to generate a singing avatar using your own audio file. You can record you voice or upload a pre recorded audio file.

![Generate from Audio](../../images/generate-from-audio.png)

### Step 9: Preview Your Uploaded Audio

After uploading your audio, **Preview** your audio to hear your uploaded audio. 

![Preview Audio](../../images/preview-audio.png)

### Step 10: Update Output Settings

After previewing your audio, **Update** the output settings if needed. You can update the following settings:

* Aspect Ratio as per your need
* Resolution upto 4K

![Update Output Settings](../../images/update-output-settings.png)

### Step 11: Click on Action Prompt

Click on **Action Prompt** to set the expression and actions of the singing avatar. 

In Action Prompt, you can enter the action and expression of the avatar like:

* Happy & emotional
* Happy & winking
* Smiling & winking
* smiling
* etc.

![Action Prompt](../../images/action-prompt.png)

### Step 12: Select or Write an Action Prompt 

Select or write an action prompt as per your need. You can also add custom action prompt as per your need.

![Action Prompt Selection](../../images/action-prompt-box.png)

### Step 13: Generate Your Singing Avatar Video

Once you are ready with all the settings, click on **Generate** button to generate your singing avatar video.

![Singing Video Generation](../../images/singing-video-generation.png)

### Step 14: Preview the Generated Audio

Before generating the final video, Zoice creates an audio preview. After the generation is complete, preview your singing AI avatar audio. 

![Generated Audio](../../images/generated-audio.png)

Listen to the preview to check:

Audio quality
Voice timing
Overall output

If everything looks good, click Generate Avatar Video to create your final singing AI video.

Note: In right top conner of the script box enchnace audio is there you toggle and it will directry generate the ai singing avatar video with enchanced audio.

### Step 15: Download Your Singing AI Avatar Video

Once you are satisfied with the generated result, click **Download**.

Save the video to your computer so it is ready to publish or share.

![Download Video](../../images/download-ai-avatar-singing-video.png)
