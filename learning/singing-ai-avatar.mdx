## What is a Singing AI Avatar?

A singing AI avatar is a digital presenter animated to lip-sync and perform in perfect synchronization with a vocal music track. By combining Zoice's advanced facial animation models with audio uploads, you can create music videos, brand jingles, or fun social media content using custom or stock avatars.

## Prerequisites

Before starting, ensure you have:
- A front-facing avatar image (or a configured custom/stock avatar).
- A clear audio vocal track (MP3 or WAV format). For best results, use clean vocals with minimal background music or instrumentation.

## Step-by-Step Guide

<Steps>
<Step title="Navigate to AI Avatar Videos">
Log in to your [Zoice Dashboard](https://zoice.com) and click **See all tools**. Scroll down and locate **AI Avatar Videos**, then click **Generate**.
<Frame>
![Select AI Avatar Videos](../../images/ca-2.png)
</Frame>
</Step>

<Step title="Select Your Avatar character">
Click on the **Avatar Character** option to open your avatar library. You can choose from **Stock Avatars** or use a **Custom Avatar** you created previously.
<Frame>
![Choose Avatar Character](../../images/ca-3.png)
</Frame>
</Step>

<Step title="Upload Your Singing Vocal Track">
In the configuration panel, switch to the audio settings tab. Click the **Upload** or **New** button under the voice section to upload your pre-recorded singing vocal track (MP3/WAV format).
<Frame>
![Upload Singing Audio](../../images/add-voice-sample.png)
</Frame>
</Step>

<Step title="Adjust Animation & Lip-Sync Settings">
For singing avatars, set the **Expression Intensity** to **High** or **Expressive** to ensure dynamic facial expressions that match the energy of the song. Choose an appropriate aspect ratio (e.g., 9:16 for TikTok/Shorts or 16:9 for YouTube).
<Frame>
![Configure Video Settings](../../images/ca-11.png)
</Frame>
</Step>

<Step title="Generate and Preview the Singing Video">
Review your settings and click the **Generate** button. Once processing is complete, download your singing AI avatar video or share it directly!
<Frame>
![Generate Video](../../images/generate-video.png)
</Frame>
</Step>
</Steps>

## Best Practices for Singing Avatars

<AccordionGroup>
<Accordion title="Vocal Audio Quality">
- **Use Clean Vocals**: Background instruments can sometimes confuse the lip-syncing engine. For best results, upload an acapella/isolated vocal track.
- **Avoid Overlapping Vocals**: Make sure only one person is singing at a time in the audio track.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Image Selection for Custom Avatars">
- **Front-Facing Angle**: Ensure the avatar's face directly faces the camera.
- **Closed Mouth**: Choose a starting image where the subject's mouth is completely closed for natural-looking lip movements.
- **Good Lighting**: Bright, even lighting helps the AI track and animate facial muscles realistically.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Fine-Tuning Expressions">
- Use the **Expression Intensity** slider to add energy.
- Match the avatar's preset emotions (e.g., Happy, Passionate) to the mood of the song for a more believable performance.
</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
