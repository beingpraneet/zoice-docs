Delivering news, corporate updates, or daily briefings requires a polished, authoritative presence. Historically, creating a news broadcast involved building a studio set, hiring professional anchors, setting up teleprompters, and spending hours on video production.

With artificial intelligence, this barrier to entry is completely gone. Using AI avatar technology, content creators, journalists, and corporate communicators can generate realistic news anchor videos directly from a typed script. 

Instead of reading off a teleprompter yourself, you can create a digital news presenter that speaks with perfect articulation, professional facial expressions, and natural pacing. 

In this article, you will learn how to create an AI news anchor video using Zoice by following a complete step-by-step process.

## What Are AI News Anchor Videos?

AI news anchor videos are broadcast-style videos generated using artificial intelligence. They feature a realistic AI avatar—often styled in professional attire behind a news desk or in a studio—reading a script with an authoritative, clear voice.

You simply write the script (the "news copy"), and the AI handles the performance, animating the avatar to deliver the news naturally.

For example, you can create an AI avatar video for:

*“Good morning, here are the top headlines for today…”*
or
*“In company news, our Q3 earnings have exceeded expectations…”*

The AI avatar delivers the script professionally, making it an excellent tool for digital journalism, company newsletters, and daily social media updates.

## How to Create an AI News Anchor Video Using Zoice

Creating a news broadcast with an AI anchor is simple. Follow the steps below to create a professional news video.

### Step 1: Visit Zoice.com and Click Sign In

Go to [Zoice.com](https://zoice.com) in your browser and click the **Sign In** button available at the top-right corner of the homepage. This will open the login page where you can access your Zoice account.

![Zoice Homepage](/images/homepage.png)

### Step 2: Sign In to Your Zoice Account

If you already have a Zoice account, enter your registered email address and password, then click **Sign In**.

You can also choose **Sign in with Google** if your account is connected with Google. After successfully signing in, you will be redirected to the Zoice dashboard.

![Sign In](/images/sign-in.png)

### Step 3: Create a New Zoice Account (For New Users)

If you are using Zoice for the first time, click **Sign Up** to create a new account.

![Sign Up](/images/sign-up.png)

Enter the required details:
- Name
- Email address
- Password

You can also select **Sign up with Google** to quickly create your account. Once your account is created, you can access the Zoice dashboard and start creating AI news videos.

### Step 4: Navigate to Avatar Characters

After logging in, you will reach the Zoice dashboard. From the left sidebar, click **Avatar Characters** under the Manage section.

![Avatar Characters Dashboard](/images/avatar-characters.png)

This section allows you to create, customize, and manage AI avatars that you can use for news broadcasting, corporate updates, and other AI-generated video content.

### Step 5: Click Create New Avatar

On the Avatar Characters page, click the **Create New** button to start creating your custom AI news anchor.

![Create New Avatar](/images/ca-4.png)

This will open the avatar creation page where you can create your avatar by uploading an image or generating one using an AI prompt.

### Step 6A: Create Your Avatar Using an Image (Skip to step 6B if you don't have an image)

Upload a clear, high-quality image of a host or presenter that you want to convert into a news anchor. Add an avatar name so you can easily identify it later in your avatar library.

![Upload Image](/images/ca-5.png)

For better results, use an image with:
- Clear face visibility
- Professional attire
- Front-facing angle
- High image quality

### Step 6B: Generate Avatar Using AI Prompt (Skip if you uploaded an image)

If you don't have an image, select the **AI Prompt** option to create an avatar using a text description. Enter an Avatar Name, describe the type of news anchor you want (for example: *"A professional male news anchor in a sharp navy suit, sitting behind a modern news desk, broadcast studio lighting"*), and click **Generate Image**. Once you are happy with the generated image, click **Generate Avatar**.

![AI Prompt](/images/ca-51.png)

### Step 7: Upload Your Avatar Image and Generate Avatar

Now upload the image you want to convert into your AI news anchor. Add a unique **Avatar Name** so you can easily find and manage your avatar later, and click **Generate Avatar**. Zoice will process your image and create your AI avatar profile.

![Generate Avatar](/images/news-anchor-upload-image.png)

*Note: The images referenced are examples; your interface will reflect your uploaded anchor image.*

### Step 8: Select Your Created Avatar

After your AI avatar profile is generated, go back to the Avatar Characters section. Your newly created avatar will appear in the avatar library. Click on the avatar you created to select it.

![Select Avatar](/images/news-anchor-select-avatar.png)

### Step 9: Go to Avatar settings

Click on Avatar Setting to add details about the AI Anchor, like Gender, Ethnicity, Age, Voice, and Accent.

![Avatar Settings](/images/news-anchor-avatar-settings.png)

### Step 10: Click Generate Video

After selecting the avatar you want to use for your broadcast from the library, click **Generate Video** to open the video creation workspace page.

![Generate Video](/images/news-anchor-generate-video-option.png)

### Step 11: Add Script and Configure Video Settings

After opening the avatar video creator, you can customize your video by entering a script, selecting a voice, choosing the output format, and adding an action prompt.

![Click on Script box to add Script](/images/news-anchor-click-on-script-box.png)

### Step 12: Add Your Script and Choose Voice Model

Click on the script box. A popup window will appear where you can write the detailed news script. Add the script that your AI anchor will speak in the broadcast. Choose the Voice Model from the right dropdown, and click on Add Script.

![Add Script](/images/news-anchor-add-script.png)

*Tip: For news broadcasts, keep the script formal, clear, and objective. Ensure sentences are structured for easy reading.*

### Step 13: Click on Select Voice

Click on the **Select Voice** option. You can browse through the available public voices, listen to voice previews, or upload your own voice. Select a formal, authoritative, and articulate AI voice model that matches a professional news broadcast tone.

![Select Voice](/images/news-anchor-click-on-select-voice.png)

  * **Choose the Voice for your AI news anchor**

    ![Choose Voice](/images/news-anchor-choose-voice.png)

### Step 14: Choose Video Output Settings

Click on **Output Settings**. Choose the aspect ratio and resolution based on where you want to publish your news video:
- **Aspect Ratio:** 16:9 for YouTube videos, website embeds, or LinkedIn, or 9:16 for TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts (perfect for quick news updates).
- **Resolution:** Select 1080p or up to 4K for high-quality professional output.

![Update Output Settings](/images/news-anchor-output-settings.png)

### Step 15: Add Action Prompt

Click on **Action Prompt** to control how your AI avatar behaves during the broadcast. You can write your own instructions or select suggested action styles (such as: *"serious and professional expression, subtle nods, maintaining strong eye contact with the camera"*).

![Click on Action Prompt](/images/news-anchor-click-on-action-prompt.png)

* **Now add action prompt for the avatar.** 

![Action Prompt](/images/news-anchor-action-prompt.png)

### Step 16: Generate Your AI News Anchor Video

After setting up the script, voice, output settings, and action prompt, review all your configuration options. Click **Generate** to start processing your AI news video.

![Generate Video](/images/news-anchor-video-generation.png)

### Step 17: Preview Audio and Click on Generate Avatar Video

Before generating the final video, Zoice creates an audio preview so you can verify how your script sounds. Check pronunciation, clarity, and pacing. If everything sounds correct, click **Generate Avatar Video** to create the final talking video.

![Generated Audio Preview](/images/news-anchor-audio-preview.png)

### Step 18: Preview and Download Your AI News Anchor Video

Once the AI avatar video finishes processing, play the final result to check the avatar movements, voice synchronization, and video quality.

![Download Video](/images/download-ai-news-anchor-video.png)

If you are satisfied, click **Download** to save the video to your device. Your AI news anchor video is now ready to be published as breaking news, a daily update, or a corporate announcement!

## Tips for Creating Better AI News Anchor Videos

* **Use Professional Backgrounds:** A news desk, a city skyline, or a branded studio background gives your avatar immediate credibility.
* **Write Like a Broadcaster:** Keep sentences concise. Use an active voice and get straight to the point.
* **Select an Authoritative Voice:** Avoid overly excited or overly casual voices. Look for tones labeled "News," "Formal," or "Authoritative."
* **Keep it Brisk:** News moves fast. Adjust the speech speed slightly if the default reading pace feels too slow for a news segment.
* **Add Lower Thirds in Post:** After downloading your Zoice video, use editing software to add graphic "lower thirds" (the text banners at the bottom of the screen) for a true broadcast feel.
