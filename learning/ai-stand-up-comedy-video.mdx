Creating viral humor and engaging video content is easier than ever with artificial intelligence. You no longer need a physical stage, expensive camera gear, or professional audio equipment to share your jokes with the world.

With Zoice, you can upload any character image (like a digital avatar, illustration, or photo of a comedian), write a hilarious script, select a custom voice, and generate a realistic AI stand up comedy video with natural expressions and lip-syncing.

Whether you are a content creator looking for funny social media posts, a writer testing new comedy material, or someone wanting to create funny animations for YouTube, making an AI stand up comedy video is the perfect solution.

In this article, you will learn how to make an AI stand up comedy video using Zoice by following a complete step-by-step process.

## What is an AI Stand Up Comedy Video?

An AI stand up comedy video is a digital performance video generated using artificial intelligence. It features a realistic or stylized AI avatar acting as a comedian on stage, delivering a stand-up routine or telling jokes. 

The AI automatically analyzes your comedy script, matches the timing, and animates the avatar's lip movements, head tilts, and facial expressions (like smiling, winking, or looking surprised) to match the delivery of the jokes.

This allows anyone to bring a comedic script to life, making it highly effective for:
- YouTube Shorts, TikToks, and Instagram Reels
- Testing stand-up comedy scripts and delivery
- Creating animated comedy web series
- Producing lighthearted marketing campaigns

## How to Create an AI Stand Up Comedy Video Using Zoice

Creating an AI stand up comedy video with Zoice takes only a few minutes. Follow the detailed steps below to generate your comedy video.

### Step 1: Visit Zoice and Click Sign In

Go to the [Zoice website](https://zoice.com) and click the **Sign In** button at the top-right corner of the homepage.

![Zoice Homepage](/images/homepage.png)

### Step 2: Create a New Zoice Account (For New Users)

If you are using Zoice for the first time, click **Sign Up** to create a new account.

![Sign Up](/images/sign-up.png)

Enter your name, email, and password, or choose **Sign up with Google** to register instantly.

### Step 3: Sign In to Your Zoice Account

Once registered, sign in with your email and password or use the Google sign-in option to access the dashboard.

![Sign In](/images/sign-in.png)

### Step 4: Navigate to Avatar Characters

From the left sidebar of the Zoice dashboard, click **Avatar Characters** under the Manage section.

![Avatar Characters Dashboard](/images/avatar-characters.png)

### Step 5: Click Create New Avatar

Click the **Create New** button to open the avatar creator tool.

![Create New Avatar](/images/ca-4.png)

### Step 6: Upload Your Comedian Image and Name Your Avatar

Upload a clear, high-quality comedian image that you want to bring to life.

![Upload Comedian Image](/images/ca-5.png)

Add a **Comedian Avatar Name** to easily identify your avatar later, then click **Generate Avatar** to create your custom AI comedian avatar.

For better results, use an image with:

- Clear face visibility and forward-facing angle
- Good contrast between character and background
- Closed or neutral mouth position
- No blurry or low-quality images

    * **Upload Your Image or Generate an Avatar Using AI Prompt**

    Zoice gives you two options to create your comedian avatar:

    - **Upload Image:** Upload a clear comedian image directly from your device.
    - **AI Prompt:** If you don't have an image, select the **AI Prompt** option and describe the comedian you want to create. Zoice will generate a custom AI image based on your prompt.

    ![AI Prompt Option](/images/ca-51.png)

    * **We upload the image of our comedian.**

    ![Upload Image](/images/ai-comedian-used-image.png)

After adding your image, enter a unique avatar name and click **Generate Avatar**.

### Step 7: Click Generate Video

Once you selected your comedian avatar, click the **Generate Video** button to open the video generation settings panel.

![Generate Video](/images/comedy-generate-video-option.png)

### Step 8: Add Your Comedy Script

In the video generation panel, Click on script box the popup window will appear here we write the detailed script also select the voice model and audio tag like emotion accent, delivery and then click on Add Script.

![Add Script](/images/comedy-click-on-script-box.png)

    * **Once the window pops up then we have to write the script in the script box and also select the voice model and audio tag like emotion accent, delivery and then click on Add Script.**

![Add Script Details](/images/comedy-add-script-details.png)

### Step 9: Click on Select Voice

After adding the script, Click on Select Voice option, and you will see few public voice and also you can upload your own voice for comedian avatar.

![Select Voice](/images/comedy-select-voice.png)

### Step 10: Choose a Voice for Your Comedian Avatar

Browse through the available voices and click on the one that matches your comedian's style. You can preview each voice before selecting it to make sure it fits your comedian's personality.

![Choose a Voice](/images/comedy-choose-voice.png)

### Step 11: Choose Output Settings

After selecting a voice, customize the video output settings based on your platform needs:

- **Aspect Ratio:** 16:9 for YouTube, 9:16 for TikTok or Instagram Reels, 1:1 for social media posts
- **Resolution:** Up to 4K for high-quality output

![Update Output Settings](/images/comedy-update-output-settings.png)

### Step 12: Add an Action Prompt

Click on **Action Prompt** to set the expression and animated actions of your comedian character. Select or write a custom action prompt that matches the mood and tone of your video content.

![Action Prompt](/images/comedy-action-prompt.png)

* **We have written a prompt for our comedian avatar.**

![Action Prompt Added](/images/comedy-action-prompt-added.png)

### Step 13: Generate Your AI Stand Up Comedy Video

Once all your settings are ready, click the **Generate** button. Zoice will process your script and comedian avatar to create the talking video.

![Generate Video](/images/comedy-final-generate-video.png)

### Step 14: Preview the Audio and Generate the Video

Before generating the final video, Zoice creates an audio preview. Listen carefully to check:

- Voice quality and clarity
- Timing and pacing of the speech
- Overall tone and expression

![Generated Audio](/images/comedy-generated-audio.png)

If everything sounds good, click **Generate Avatar Video** to create your final stand up comedy video.

### Step 15: Wait for Your Comedian Avatar Video to Process

Zoice will now process and render your stand up comedy video. Processing time may vary depending on the length of your script and selected resolution. You can stay on the page or return later to check on the progress.

![Generating Video](/images/comedy-generating-video.png)

### Step 16: Preview and Download Your AI Stand Up Comedy Video

Once your video is ready, preview it to confirm the lip-sync quality, voice match, and overall output.

![Download Video](/images/comedy-download-video.png)
