## Writing Effective Prompts

### Prompt Structure

A well-structured prompt typically includes:

1. **Subject**: What is the main focus?
2. **Style**: What artistic style or aesthetic?
3. **Details**: Colors, lighting, composition
4. **Quality**: Resolution, detail level descriptors

### Example Prompts

<CodeGroup>
```text Simple
A red sports car on a mountain road at sunset
```

```text Detailed
A sleek red Ferrari driving on a winding mountain road during golden hour, dramatic clouds in the sky, cinematic lighting, photorealistic, 4K quality
```

```text Artistic
An abstract watercolor painting of a city skyline, vibrant blues and purples, flowing brushstrokes, modern art style, high contrast
```

```text Product
A minimalist product photo of a smartwatch on a white marble surface, soft studio lighting, professional photography, clean composition, sharp focus
```
</CodeGroup>

## Generation Parameters

### Resolution Options
- **Standard**: 1024x1024 (fastest)
- **High**: 1536x1536 (balanced)
- **Ultra**: 2048x2048 (highest quality)

### Aspect Ratios
- **Square**: 1:1 (social media, profile pictures)
- **Portrait**: 3:4 or 9:16 (vertical content)
- **Landscape**: 16:9 or 4:3 (horizontal content)
- **Wide**: 21:9 (cinematic)

### Advanced Settings

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Guidance Scale">
    Controls how closely the AI follows your prompt
    - **Low (1-5)**: More creative, less literal
    - **Medium (6-10)**: Balanced interpretation
    - **High (11-15)**: Strict adherence to prompt
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Seed">
    Use a specific seed number for reproducible results
    - Leave blank for random generation
    - Use same seed to recreate similar images
    - Useful for iterating on a concept
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Number of Images">
    Generate multiple variations
    - 1-4 images per generation
    - Each variation is unique
    - Helps find the best result
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Prompt Tips by Category

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Photography">
    **Key Elements:**
    - Camera type and lens
    - Lighting conditions
    - Composition style
    - Depth of field
    
    **Example:**
    "Professional portrait photo, 85mm lens, soft natural lighting, shallow depth of field, bokeh background"
  </Tab>
  
  <Tab title="Art Styles">
    **Popular Styles:**
    - Oil painting
    - Watercolor
    - Digital art
    - Anime/Manga
    - Sketch/Line art
    
    **Example:**
    "Oil painting in the style of impressionism, visible brushstrokes, vibrant colors, outdoor scene"
  </Tab>
  
  <Tab title="3D Renders">
    **Key Elements:**
    - Rendering engine
    - Material properties
    - Lighting setup
    - Level of detail
    
    **Example:**
    "3D render, Octane render, metallic materials, studio lighting, highly detailed, 8K resolution"
  </Tab>
  
  <Tab title="Concepts">
    **Key Elements:**
    - Mood and atmosphere
    - Color palette
    - Abstract elements
    - Symbolic meaning
    
    **Example:**
    "Abstract concept of innovation, flowing energy, blue and gold colors, futuristic, dynamic composition"
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Image doesn't match prompt">
    - Make your prompt more specific
    - Increase guidance scale
    - Try a different model
    - Regenerate with variations
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Low quality results">
    - Increase resolution setting
    - Add quality keywords to prompt
    - Use a higher-tier model
    - Specify "detailed" or "sharp focus"
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Unexpected elements">
    - Simplify your prompt
    - Remove contradictory descriptions
    - Be more explicit about what to exclude
    - Try negative prompts if available
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Tip>
Save prompts that work well! Build a library of effective prompts for different use cases.
