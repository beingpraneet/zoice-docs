## What is Image to Avatar?

Image to Avatar allows you to create AI-generated characters from your uploaded photos. These avatars maintain the likeness of the person in the photo while adding realistic animations, lip-sync, and expressions for video presentations.

## Benefits of Image to Avatar

- **Personal Branding**: Use your own face or brand representatives
- **Authenticity**: Real people, AI-powered animation
- **Brand Consistency**: Maintain visual brand identity
- **Professional Presence**: Create polished video content
- **Flexibility**: Multiple team members as avatars
- **Reusable**: Create once, use for multiple videos

## Getting Started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to New Avatar">
    Go to the **New Avatar** section in the left menu of your Zoice dashboard.
    <Frame>
      ![Navigate to New Avatar](../images/new-avatar.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Manage Custom Avatar">
    Select the **Manage Custom Avatar** option to start creating your personalized character.
    <Frame>
      ![Manage Custom Avatar](../images/manage-custom-avatar.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create New">
    Click on the **Create New** button to begin the avatar creation process.
    <Frame>
      ![Create New](../images/create-new.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Upload Image">
    Use your own high-quality, front-facing photo to create your avatar's base image.
    <Frame>
      ![Upload Image](../images/upload-image.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Name Your Avatar">
    Enter a descriptive name for your avatar to easily identify it in your gallery later.
    <Frame>
      ![Name Your Avatar](../images/name-your-avatar.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Click on Generate Avatar">
    Once you've added your image, click on **Generate Avatar**. You will now see your avatar saved in your gallery with that image.
    <Frame>
      ![Upload image to generate avatar](../images/upload-ai-avatar-image.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Select Saved Avatar">
    Click on your saved avatar from the custom avatar gallery to select it.
    <Frame>
      ![Select Saved Avatar](../images/custom-avatar-gallery.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Navigate to Settings">
    Once selected, click to navigate to the settings to begin your customization.
    <Frame>
      ![Navigate to Settings](../images/navigate-to-settings.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Update Profile Details">
    Fine-tune your avatar's profile by adjusting settings like **Age**, **Ethnicity**, and **Gender** to match your vision.
    <Frame>
      ![Update Profile](../images/configure-profile-settings.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Go Back to New Avatar Section and Select Custom Avatar">
    Go back to the **New Avatar** section and select **Custom Avatar**. 
    <Frame>
      ![Go Back to New Avatar Section and Select Custom Avatar](../images/go-back-to-new-avatar-section-and-select-custom-avatar.png)
    </Frame>
    From here:
    - **Select Base Image**: Choose your newly customized avatar image from the gallery.
    <Frame>
      ![Select Base Image](../images/select-base-image.png)
    </Frame>
    - **Click Add Voice**: Select a voice or upload your own voice sample.
    <Frame>
      ![Add Voice](../images/add-voice-sample.png)
    </Frame>
    - **Upload Voice Sample**: Upload your voice sample want use for avatar.
    <Frame>
      ![Upload Voice Sample](../images/add-voice-sample-1.png)
    </Frame>
    - **Add Action**: Define the action you want your avatar to perform like greeting, waving, etc.
    <Frame>
      ![Add Action](../images/add-action.png)
    </Frame>
    - **Resolution**: Choose your preferred video resolution.
    <Frame>
      ![Resolution](../images/select-resolution.png)
    </Frame>
    - **Write Script**: Enter the text you want your avatar to speak in the script box.
    <Frame>
      ![Write Script](../images/add-your-script.png)
    </Frame>
    - **Generate**: Click on **Generate** to create your final video.
    <Frame>
      ![Generate Video](../images/generate-custom-avatar1.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Customization Options

### Appearance Controls
- **Age Range**: Child, teen, young adult, middle-aged, senior
- **Gender**: Male, female, non-binary
- **Ethnicity**: Various ethnic backgrounds available
- **Hair**: Color, length, style
- **Facial Features**: Eyes, nose, mouth, facial structure
- **Build**: Body type and proportions

### Voice Integration
Combine your image-generated avatar with:
- Custom voice selection
- Voice cloning for unique voices
- Emotion and tone control
- Multi-language support

### Animation Control
- **Expression Intensity**: Subtle to expressive
- **Movement Style**: Static, moderate, or dynamic
- **Gesture Options**: Hand movements and body language
- **Camera Angles**: Front, slight angle, or dynamic

## Best Practices

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Image Selection Tips">
    - Use a clear, high-resolution front-facing photo
    - Ensure good lighting on the face
    - Avoid hats or large sunglasses
    - Use a neutral background if possible
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Consistency">
    - Document your avatar specifications
    - Create style guides for brand avatars
    - Use the same voice for the same character
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Tip>
For the best results, use a photo with a neutral expression and straightforward gaze.
</Tip>

<Warning>
Generated avatars should not be used to impersonate real individuals without consent.
</Warning>
