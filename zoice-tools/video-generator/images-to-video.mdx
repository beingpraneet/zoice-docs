## Overview

Images-to-Video allows you to create video content by combining multiple images into a single video sequence. Perfect for slideshows, transitions, storytelling, or creating videos from image sequences.

## How It Works

Upload multiple images and Zoice will combine them into a video, with options for transitions, timing, and effects. You can also add AI-generated transitions and motion between images.

## Quick Start

<Steps>
  <Step title="Upload Images">
    Select 2-10 images in the order you want them
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Transition Style">
    Choose how images transition between each other
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure Timing">
    Set duration for each image
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Effects">
    Optional: Add motion, zoom, or pan effects
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate">
    Create your video

    Save the final video

## Image Requirements

### Technical Specifications
- **Number of Images**: 2-10 images per video
- **Formats**: JPG, PNG, WebP
- **Max Size**: 10MB per image
- **Recommended Resolution**: 1920x1080 or higher
- **Aspect Ratio**: Consistent across all images recommended

### Content Guidelines
- Related images work best for cohesive videos
- Similar lighting and style create smoother transitions
- Consistent aspect ratios prevent cropping
- High-quality images produce better results

## Transition Styles

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Cut">

  <Tab title="Fade">

  </Tab>
  
  <Tab title="Slide">

    - Best for: Product showcases, galleries
  </Tab>
  
  <Tab title="AI Morph">

    - Best for: Artistic videos, transformations

## Timing Settings

### Per-Image Duration
- **Fast**: 1-2 seconds per image
- **Medium**: 3-4 seconds per image
- **Slow**: 5-6 seconds per image
- **Custom**: Set individual timing for each image

### Transition Duration
- **Quick**: 0.3-0.5 seconds
- **Standard**: 0.8-1.0 seconds
- **Slow**: 1.5-2.0 seconds

## Tips for Different Content Types

### Photography Portfolios
- Use fade transitions
- 4-5 seconds per image
- Subtle Ken Burns effect
- Consistent pacing

### Product Catalogs
- Slide transitions
- 3 seconds per image
- Slight zoom on product
- Clean, professional

### Event Highlights
- Mix of cuts and fades
- Variable timing (2-4 seconds)
- Dynamic effects
- Energetic pacing

### Educational Content
- Simple transitions
- Longer duration (5-6 seconds)
- Minimal effects
- Clear, focused

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Inconsistent quality">
    - Use images of similar resolution
    - Ensure all images are high quality
    - Match aspect ratios
    - Avoid mixing portrait and landscape
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Jarring transitions">
    - Increase transition duration
    - Use softer transition styles
    - Ensure images are related
    - Reduce motion effects
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Video too short/long">
    - Adjust per-image duration
    - Add or remove images
    - Modify transition lengths
    - Use custom timing
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
