## Writing Video Prompts

### Essential Elements

A good video prompt includes:

1. **Subject/Scene**: What's happening
2. **Action/Movement**: How things move
3. **Camera Work**: Camera angles and movements
4. **Lighting/Atmosphere**: Mood and lighting
5. **Style**: Visual aesthetic

### Example Prompts

<CodeGroup>
```text Simple
A cat playing with a ball of yarn in a sunny room
```

```text Detailed
A fluffy orange cat playfully batting at a red ball of yarn on a hardwood floor, sunlight streaming through a window creating warm shadows, camera slowly panning right, cozy home atmosphere
```

```text Cinematic
Aerial drone shot flying over a misty mountain valley at sunrise, camera moving forward and descending, golden light breaking through clouds, cinematic color grading, 4K quality
```

```text Product
Close-up shot of a smartphone rotating on a white surface, studio lighting with soft shadows, camera orbiting around the product, professional commercial style, clean and minimal
```
</CodeGroup>

## Video Parameters

### Duration
- **Short**: 2-3 seconds (quick clips)
- **Medium**: 4-6 seconds (standard)
- **Long**: 7-10 seconds (detailed scenes)

<Note>
Longer videos take more time to generate and may cost more credits.
</Note>

### Resolution
- **720p**: Fast generation, smaller file size
- **1080p**: High quality, recommended for most uses

### Motion Intensity
- **Low**: Subtle movements, static scenes
- **Medium**: Moderate action and camera movement
- **High**: Dynamic action, fast camera movements

## Camera Movement Keywords

Use these keywords to control camera work:

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Camera Angles">
    - **Close-up**: Tight shot of subject
    - **Wide shot**: Full scene view
    - **Aerial view**: Bird's eye perspective
    - **Low angle**: Camera looking up
    - **High angle**: Camera looking down
    - **Eye level**: Straight-on view
  </Tab>
  
  <Tab title="Camera Movements">
    - **Pan left/right**: Horizontal rotation
    - **Tilt up/down**: Vertical rotation
    - **Dolly in/out**: Move toward/away
    - **Tracking shot**: Follow subject
    - **Orbit**: Circle around subject
    - **Crane shot**: Vertical movement
  </Tab>
  
  <Tab title="Camera Effects">
    - **Slow motion**: Slowed action
    - **Time-lapse**: Sped up time
    - **Zoom in/out**: Lens zoom
    - **Rack focus**: Focus shift
    - **Handheld**: Shaky, dynamic feel
    - **Steady cam**: Smooth, stable
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Lighting & Atmosphere

### Lighting Conditions
- **Golden hour**: Warm, soft sunset/sunrise light
- **Blue hour**: Cool twilight tones
- **Studio lighting**: Professional, controlled
- **Natural light**: Realistic outdoor lighting
- **Dramatic lighting**: High contrast, shadows
- **Soft lighting**: Diffused, even illumination

### Weather & Atmosphere
- **Sunny**: Bright, clear conditions
- **Cloudy**: Overcast, soft light
- **Rainy**: Wet surfaces, rain effects
- **Foggy**: Misty, atmospheric
- **Stormy**: Dramatic, dark clouds
- **Snowy**: Winter conditions

## Best Practices

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Prompt Writing Tips">
    - Start with the main subject and action
    - Add camera movement for dynamic videos
    - Include lighting and atmosphere details
    - Specify style and mood
    - Keep prompts focused and clear
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Action Description">
    - Use active verbs (running, flying, spinning)
    - Describe speed and intensity
    - Mention direction of movement
    - Include interaction between elements
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Scene Composition">
    - Describe foreground and background
    - Mention key visual elements
    - Include environmental details
    - Specify color palette if important
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Common Use Cases

### Marketing & Advertising
```text
Product showcase video, smartphone rotating on pedestal, 
studio lighting, camera orbiting smoothly, professional 
commercial style, clean white background
```

### Social Media Content
```text
Trendy lifestyle shot, coffee being poured into a cup, 
steam rising, morning sunlight, camera slow motion, 
Instagram aesthetic, warm tones

golden hour lighting, cinematic nature documentary style

Colorful liquid paint swirling and mixing, vibrant colors

```

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Video doesn't match prompt">
    - Make prompt more specific
    - Add more descriptive details
    - Try different model
    - Simplify complex scenes
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Choppy or unnatural motion">
    - Adjust motion intensity
    - Simplify camera movements
    - Use smoother action descriptions
    - Try different duration
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Low quality output">
    - Increase resolution to 1080p
    - Use quality keywords (4K, cinematic, detailed)
    - Try a higher-tier model
    - Ensure prompt is clear and focused
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Tip>
Start with shorter videos (2-4 seconds) to test your prompts, then increase duration once you're happy with the results.
</Tip>

## Advanced Techniques

### Combining Multiple Elements
```text
[Subject] + [Action] + [Camera Movement] + [Lighting] + [Style]

Example: A red sports car driving on coastal highway, 
speeding along curves, aerial drone following from above, 
sunset golden hour lighting, cinematic commercial style
```

### Creating Loops
For looping videos, describe cyclical actions:
```text
Ocean waves gently rolling onto sandy beach, continuous 
motion, camera static, soft natural lighting, seamless loop
```
