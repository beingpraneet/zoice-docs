## Overview

Convert static images into dynamic, speaking AI avatars. Upload a photo and create a lifelike avatar that can present content, deliver messages, or engage with your audience through realistic animations and speech.

## Image Requirements

### Technical Specifications
- **Format**: JPG, PNG, WebP
- **Resolution**: Minimum 512x512px, recommended 1024x1024px+
- **File Size**: Maximum 10MB
- **Aspect Ratio**: Square (1:1) or portrait (3:4) preferred

### Content Requirements
- **Face Visibility**: Clear, unobstructed frontal view
- **Lighting**: Well-lit, minimal shadows on face
- **Expression**: Neutral or slight smile recommended
- **Background**: Simple, non-distracting
- **Quality**: Sharp focus, high resolution
- **Single Subject**: One face per image

<Warning>
Avoid images with sunglasses, masks, heavy makeup, or extreme angles for best results.
</Warning>

- **720p**: Fast rendering, web-friendly

- **MP4**: Universal compatibility

## Best Practices

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Photo Selection">

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Poor lip sync">
    - Use clearer source image
    - Simplify script
    - Adjust voice speed
    - Try different voice
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Unnatural movements">
    - Reduce animation intensity
    - Use more neutral source image
    - Try different animation style
    - Check image quality
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Low quality output">
    - Use higher resolution source image
    - Increase output resolution
    - Ensure good lighting in source
    - Check image sharpness
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Tip>
For the most realistic results, use a high-quality, professionally-lit photo with a neutral expression and simple background.
</Tip>
