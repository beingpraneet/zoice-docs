## Overview

Create unique AI avatars from scratch using text descriptions. Design custom characters with specific appearances, styles, and personalities for your video content without needing photos or images.

## Quick Start

<Steps>
  <Step title="Write Description">
    Describe the avatar's appearance and characteristics
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Style">
    Choose realistic, stylized, or cartoon style
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate Avatar">
    AI creates your custom avatar (30-90 seconds)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review & Refine">
    Adjust description if needed and regenerate
  </Step>

  </Step>

  </Step>

    Create your avatar presentation

## Writing Avatar Descriptions

### Essential Elements

A complete avatar description includes:

1. **Demographics**: Age, gender, ethnicity
2. **Physical Features**: Hair, eyes, facial features
3. **Style**: Clothing, accessories, overall aesthetic
4. **Expression**: Mood, personality, demeanor
5. **Background**: Setting or environment

### Example Descriptions

<CodeGroup>
```text Professional Business
A professional woman in her mid-30s with shoulder-length 
brown hair, wearing a navy blue blazer and white blouse, 
confident smile, warm brown eyes, modern office background, 
professional and approachable demeanor
```

```text Tech Presenter
A young tech professional in their late 20s, casual style 
with glasses, short dark hair, wearing a branded t-shirt, 
friendly and enthusiastic expression, modern studio background, 
innovative and energetic personality
```

```text Educational Instructor
A friendly teacher in their 40s with graying hair, wearing 
smart casual attire, warm smile, patient expression, 
classroom or library background, knowledgeable and 
approachable presence
```

```text Creative Character
A creative artist in their 20s with colorful dyed hair, 
unique fashion sense, expressive features, artistic tattoos, 
vibrant studio background, bold and imaginative personality
```

```text Cartoon Mascot
A friendly cartoon character with big expressive eyes, 
bright smile, colorful outfit, playful personality, 
simple background, fun and engaging style
```
</CodeGroup>

## Advanced Prompt Techniques

### Layered Descriptions

Build detailed prompts in layers:

```text
Base: A professional presenter in their 30s
+ Appearance: Short dark hair, glasses, friendly smile
+ Clothing: Navy blazer, white shirt, modern style
+ Setting: Bright office with plants in background
+ Mood: Confident and approachable, professional demeanor
```

### Style References

Reference specific styles:

```text
"In the style of modern corporate photography"
"Pixar-style 3D animated character"
"Professional LinkedIn profile aesthetic"
"Vibrant YouTube thumbnail style"
```

### Negative Prompts

Specify what to avoid:

```text
Avatar description: [your description]
Avoid: Extreme expressions, busy backgrounds, 
distracting accessories, overly formal appearance
```

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Avatar doesn't match description">
    - Make description more specific
    - Simplify complex requests
    - Try different style option
    - Regenerate with adjusted prompt
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Inconsistent results">
    - Use more detailed descriptions
    - Save successful prompts
    - Maintain consistent terminology
    - Specify style clearly
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Unnatural appearance">
    - Simplify description
    - Avoid contradictory details
    - Try different style
    - Use reference style keywords
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Tip>
Start with simple descriptions and gradually add details. Too many specific requirements at once can sometimes produce unexpected results.
</Tip>

<Warning>
Generated avatars should not be used to impersonate real individuals. Ensure ethical use of AI-generated characters.
